## 1. Statutory Regulatory Compliance Framework

### 1.1 FSSAI Mandates (Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006)
All commercial production, processing, packaging, import, and distribution must strictly conform to the following regulations promulgated under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006:

#### A. Food Safety and Standards (Food Products Standards and Food Additives) Regulations, 2011
**Category 2.4.6: "Pulses" and "Split Pulse (Dal)":**
Toor Dal (Arhar Dal / Pigeon Pea, *Cajanus cajan*) is classified as a whole, dehusked, or split grain of leguminous origin. The product must be sound, clean, mature, sweet, wholesome, and free from injurious contaminants, foreign matter, musty odor, molds, and living insects.

| Parameter | Statutory FSSAI Limit | FinnoAQ Engineered Spec | Process Compliance & Control Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Moisture Content** | Max $14.0\%\ (\text{w/w})$ | $10.5\% - 11.2\%\ (\text{w/w})$ | Infrared drying stage cools and stabilizes product well below water activity ($a_w < 0.60$) boundary, preventing mold germination. |
| **Extraneous Matter (Inorganic)** | Max $0.2\%\ (\text{w/w})$ | $< 0.02\%\ (\text{w/w})$ | Multi-stage vibratory de-stoner, gravity separator, and rare-earth magnetic grids (10,000 Gauss). |
| **Extraneous Matter (Organic)** | Max $0.8\%\ (\text{w/w})$ | $< 0.05\%\ (\text{w/w})$ | High-capacity aspirator cyclones remove chaff, straw, and foreign botanical matter. |
| **Damaged / Discolored Grains** | Max $3.0\%\ (\text{w/w})$ | $< 0.50\%\ (\text{w/w})$ | 64-channel trichromatic optical color sorter (OPT-101) with infrared inspection sensors. |
| **Weevilled Grains (by count)** | Max $1.0\%$ | $0.0\%$ | Continuous infrared thermal shock ($180-185^\circ\text{C}$ surface) guarantees $100\%$ ovicidal and larvicidal kill. |
| **Foreign Edible Seeds** | Max $2.0\%\ (\text{w/w})$ | $< 0.10\%\ (\text{w/w})$ | Precision screen sizers (SCR-101) remove non-target pulses (chana, urad, moong). |
| **Uric Acid** | Max $100\ \text{mg/kg}$ | $< 10\ \text{mg/kg}$ | Enzymatic uricase assay verifies zero pre-process insect contamination. |
| **Aflatoxin $B_1$** | Max $10\ \mu\text{g/kg}\ (\text{ppb})$ | $< 1.0\ \mu\text{g/kg}$ | HPLC-FLD screen; dry storage condition keeps $a_w < 0.60$, halting *Aspergillus flavus* growth. |
| **Total Aflatoxins ($B_1+B_2+G_1+G_2$)** | Max $15\ \mu\text{g/kg}\ (\text{ppb})$ | $< 2.0\ \mu\text{g/kg}$ | Validated by accredited LC-MS/MS testing on every raw imported container lot. |
| **Synthetic Food Colors** | **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** | **ABSENT (0.0%)** | Zero synthetic dyes. FinnoAQ is a purely physical thermomechanical process. |

#### B. Prohibition of Adulterants & Synthetic Colorants
Under **FSSAI Regulation 2.1.1 (Prohibition of use of Artificial Colours in Pulses)**:
- The addition of synthetic food colors—most notably **Metanil Yellow** (a toxic azo dye frequently used in illicit dal polishing), Tartrazine, or Sunset Yellow—is an actionable criminal offense under Section 59 of the FSS Act (Unsafe Food).
- FinnoAQ utilizes **zero artificial colors, mineral oils, or leather polishes**. Optical color consistency is achieved solely through calibrated infrared radiant heat and seed coat detachment.

#### C. Food Additive & Clean-Label Status
- While chemical chelation using Sodium Tripolyphosphate (STPP, INS 451(i)) was screened during Gate 2 diagnosis, FinnoAQ's commercialized industrial route utilizes the **100% clean-label physical infrared thermal shock route** ($183^\circ\text{C}$ IR exposure + controlled moisture tempering).
- **Statutory Impact:** The finished product contains **no added chemicals, no preservatives, and no synthetic processing aids**, qualifying for clean-label retail claims ("100% Natural, Mechanically Processed Quick-Cooking Toor Dal").

### 1.2 Import Regulations & Plant Quarantine Compliance

Raw Tanzanian Toor Dal imported through Nhava Sheva (Jawaharlal Nehru Port Trust - JNPT) / Mumbai Port must comply with:

1. **Plant Quarantine (Regulation of Import into India) Order, 2003 (PQO 2003):**
   - Issued by Directorate of Plant Protection, Quarantine & Storage (DPPQS), Ministry of Agriculture.
   - Requires an official **Phytosanitary Certificate (PSC)** issued by the National Plant Protection Organization (NPPO) of Tanzania.
   - Mandatory endorsement of port-of-origin fumigation with **Methyl Bromide ($32\ \text{g/m}^3$ for 24 hrs at $\ge 21^\circ\text{C}$)** or **Phosphine ($2\ \text{g/m}^3$ for 7 days)**.
   - Declaration of freedom from quarantine weed seeds (*Striga hermonthica*, *Cuscuta* spp.) and pulse pests (*Callosobruchus chinensis*, *Trogoderma granarium*).

2. **FSSAI Food Import Entry Regulations, 2017:**
   - Prior to customs clearance, the importer must file a Bill of Entry on ICEGATE and submit an online application via FSSAI's **Food Import Clearance System (FICS)**.
   - FSSAI Authorized Officer (AO) conducts visual inspection, verifies seal integrity, and draws dual representative samples for testing at an FSSAI-notified laboratory at JNPT.
   - **No Objection Certificate (NOC)** is issued electronically upon satisfactory lab compliance report.

3. **Non-GMO Compliance Directive:**
   - Every imported food consignment containing pulses must be accompanied by a Non-GMO Certificate issued by the competent authority of the exporting nation, certifying that the genetically modified organism threshold is $< 1.0\%$.

## 5. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for Consignment Sampling & Release

1. **Sampling Protocol (BIS IS 2814 / ISO 24333):**
   - For bulk container arrivals ($20\ \text{ft}$ GP containers, ~24 MT dal per container), draw samples using a tiered cylindrical grain probe from 8 distinct grid locations and 3 depth levels (top, middle, bottom).
   - Minimum composite sample size: $10\ \text{kg}$ per container; homogenize via a Boerner divider to yield four $1.0\ \text{kg}$ sealed analytical portions (Lab Test, Regulatory Retain, Factory Control, Importer Arbitral).
2. **Chain of Custody & Traceability:**
   - Samples sealed with tamper-evident barcoded security tape.
   - Electronic sample logging with container number, bill of lading, seal ID, origin lot number, and GPS timestamp.
3. **Turnaround Time (TAT) & Release Gate:**
   - SGS Port physical & fumigant check: **24 hours**.
   - Eurofins pesticide, heavy metal, and aflatoxin clearance: **48-72 hours**.
   - Material is quarantined in Bonded Intake Silo until full certificate of analysis (COA) is uploaded and verified against FSSAI statutory limits.
   - Automated ERP lock prevents transfer of unreleased grain into the active processing stream.
