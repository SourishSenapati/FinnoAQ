**Project Reference:** FNQ-PR-2026-001  
**Lead Chemical & Process Engineer:** Sourish Senapati  
**Scope:** Staged Commercialization from Discovery to 15 TPD Industrial Production  
**Governance:** Truth Precedes Completeness; Gated Capital Deployment; Failsafe Stopping Rules  

---

## 1. Scale-Up Philosophy: The Stage-Gate Protocol

Scaling chemical and food processing technologies from laboratory benchtop to continuous multi-tonne industrial execution carries severe technical and commercial risks. FinnoaQ enforces an unbending **Stage-Gate Protocol**:
1. **No Capital Deployment Ahead of Data:** Capital for subsequent phases is locked until the governing technical and commercial gates of the current phase are validated by laboratory or physical pilot measurements.
2. **Quantitative Thresholds Over Qualitative Assertions:** Every gate is bounded by hard numerical pass/fail criteria (e.g., shear force in Newtons, $p$-values in sensory testing, crack percentages, cost per kilogram).
3. **Failsafe Stopping Rules:** If a fundamental physical barrier (e.g., irreversible off-flavour or severe cellular collapse) is encountered that cannot be resolved within budget and thermodynamic bounds, the project halts immediately. Capital is returned to investors rather than wasted on unviable commercial scale-up.

## 3. Detailed Phase Breakdown

### Phase 0: Evidence and Customer Discovery
- **Timeline:** Months 1–2 (8 Weeks)
- **Budget Ceiling:** **₹12.00 Lakhs** (Funded via Tranche 0 of Seed Capital)
- **Primary Objectives:**
  - Secure and chemically profile 20 representative commercial dal lots (imported African toor from Tanzania, Mozambique, Malawi, Sudan; domestic desi from Latur and Gulbarga).
  - Perform 15 in-depth, structured field discovery interviews with pulse mill owners in Latur, Akola, and Indore to confirm processing constraints, price discounts, and tolling willingness-to-pay.
  - Audit and purge all unsupported claims and software assumptions in earlier repositories, establishing an untainted, auditable physical baseline.
  - Formally resolve all 10 repository audit inquiries and document feedstock provenance.
- **Key Work Packages:**
  - *WP 0.1:* Dal sample intake, moisture determination (AOAC 925.10), phytate analysis, and free calcium/magnesium pectate titration.
  - *WP 0.2:* Miller field interviews and discovery synthesis.
  - *WP 0.3:* Baseline domestic cooker characterization and physical acoustic sensor calibration.
- **Go/No-Go Gate 0:**
  1. *Chemical Defect Confirmation:* $\ge 80\%$ of imported samples exhibit middle lamella calcium-magnesium pectate cross-linking $> 25\text{ meq/kg}$.
  2. *Commercial Demand Confirmation:* Minimum 12 of 15 interviewed millers confirm willingness to test toll processing at $\le ₹12/\text{kg}$ with guaranteed $\ge 95\%$ yield.
  3. *Audit Clearance:* All 10 repository audit questions answered without unsubstantiated claims.

### Phase 1: Controlled Bench-Scale Validation
- **Timeline:** Months 3–6 (16 Weeks)
- **Budget Ceiling:** **₹33.00 Lakhs** (Funded via Tranche 1 of Seed Capital; Total Seed Ask = **₹45.00 Lakhs**)
- **Primary Objectives:**
  - Execute controlled 1–5 kg batch reactor trials across the 14-formulation matrix.
  - Lock down S1-09 buffer concentration ($0.085\text{ M}$ Citrate, $0.025\text{ M}$ Citric Acid, $0.035\text{ M}$ SHMP, pH $5.20 \pm 0.05$).
  - Demonstrate reproducible 2-whistle tenderization ($F_{\text{WB}} \le 18.0\text{ N}$) on Hawkins 3 L benchmark cooker.
  - Execute formal ISO 4120 3-Alternative Forced Choice (3-AFC) sensory triangle testing with 30 trained panellists to establish sensory equivalence ($p > 0.05$).
  - Clear surface salts to $< 50\text{ ppm}$ and confirm internal phosphate $< 500\text{ mg/kg}$ (vs FSSAI MRL $5,000\text{ mg/kg}$).
  - Establish shortest effective pretreatment time ($t_{\text{soak}} \le 24\text{ min}$).
- **Key Work Packages:**
  - *WP 1.1:* Jacketed bench reactor optimization ($T = 40–55^\circ\text{C}$, $L/S = 1.2–2.0$).
  - *WP 1.2:* Instrumental texture profiling (Warner-Bratzler shear cell and compression testing).
  - *WP 1.3:* Formal sensory panel trials and statistical analysis.
  - *WP 1.4:* Analytical residue verification (ICP-MS for phosphorus and HPLC for citrate).
- **Go/No-Go Gate 1:**
  1. *Cookability:* Cooked shear force $F_{\text{WB}} \le 18.0\text{ N}$ after 2-whistle cycle in $\ge 95\%$ of test replicates.
  2. *Sensory Equivalence:* 3-AFC aftertaste test demonstrates no statistically significant discrimination from untreated reference ($p > 0.05$).
  3. *Food Safety:* Internal phosphate $< 500\text{ mg/kg}$ and surface salts $< 50\text{ ppm}$.
  4. *Yield:* Dry matter solids yield $\ge 97.0\%$.

### Phase 2: Engineering Pilot Skid (100 kg/h)
- **Timeline:** Months 7–12 (24 Weeks)
- **Budget Ceiling:** **₹65.00 Lakhs** (Funded via Series Seed-Extension / Pre-Series A)
- **Primary Objectives:**
  - Procure and commission an integrated 25–100 kg/batch (100 kg/h continuous) skid.
  - Validate continuous counter-current steep auger residence time distribution (RTD) and axial dispersion.
  - Validate two-stage fluid bed drying with 20-min tempering ($62^\circ\text{C} \to 45^\circ\text{C} \to 52^\circ\text{C}$).
  - Demonstrate cotyledon checking $< 2.0\%$ and broken split generation $< 1.5\%$.
  - Prove nanofiltration water recovery skid recovering $\ge 80\%$ rinse water.
  - Measure true batch-to-batch operational variability and utility consumption.
- **Key Work Packages:**
  - *WP 2.1:* Skid fabrication, erection, and mechanical FAT (Factory Acceptance Testing).
  - *WP 2.2:* Hydrodynamic and thermal commissioning; RTD tracer testing.
  - *WP 2.3:* Drying stress optimization and friability verification.
  - *WP 2.4:* Membrane filtration and closed-loop water recovery testing.
- **Go/No-Go Gate 2:**
  1. *Mechanical Quality:* Fissuring/checking rate $< 2.0\%$ and brokens $< 1.5\%$ across 10 consecutive 100 kg runs.
  2. *Cycle Time:* Total processing time from infeed to shelf-stable dry dal $\le 90\text{ minutes}$.
  3. *Water Efficiency:* Membrane permeate recovery $\ge 80\%$ with permeate TDS $< 150\text{ ppm}$.
  4. *Process Stability:* Consistent $F_{\text{WB}} \le 18.0\text{ N}$ across 5 different raw African feedstock batches.

### Phase 3: Demonstration Plant (1 TPD Partner Mill Site)
- **Timeline:** Months 13–18 (24 Weeks)
- **Budget Ceiling:** **₹120.00 Lakhs** (Funded via Series A Lead / Industrial Partner Co-Investment)
- **Primary Objectives:**
  - Install a 1.0 TPD continuous demonstration skid inside an active commercial pulse mill in Latur, Maharashtra.
  - Execute a 30-day continuous trial processing **25 metric tonnes** of commercial African HTC dal.
  - Validate overall equipment effectiveness (OEE $\ge 88\%$), CIP cleaning cycles, and mechanical reliability.
  - Establish true industrial OpEx per tonne under real mill operating conditions (mill labour, grid power, biomass steam).
  - Package and seed 25 MT of treated dal into local institutional catering (hostels, canteens) and regional wholesale mandi channels.
  - Secure signed commercial tolling or technology conversion agreement from partner mill.
- **Key Work Packages:**
  - *WP 3.1:* Site preparation, utility tie-ins, and demo plant erection.
  - *WP 3.2:* 30-day continuous operational campaign (25 MT throughput).
  - *WP 3.3:* Mill worker operating training and SOP standardization.
  - *WP 3.4:* Wholesale market seeding, buyer feedback collection, and commercial contract execution.
- **Go/No-Go Gate 3:**
  1. *Zero Consumer Rejection:* Zero commercial returns or quality rejections across the 25 MT seeded lot.
  2. *Operating Reliability:* Demonstration plant achieves $\ge 88\%$ OEE with zero catastrophic shutdowns.
  3. *Cost Verification:* Audited industrial OpEx confirmed $\le ₹7.00/\text{kg}$.
  4. *Commercial Conversion:* Partner mill executes a multi-year commercial tolling agreement or converts to a permanent licensing contract.

### Phase 4: Commercial Scale Expansion (15 TPD Production Line)
- **Timeline:** Months 19–30 (48 Weeks)
- **Budget Ceiling:** **₹350.00 Lakhs** (Funded via Series A Equity ₹200L + Equipment Debt ₹150L under AIF / PMKSY Scheme)

  - Erect and commission a standalone, centralized 15 TPD (4,500 MT/year) industrial processing facility in the Latur/Solapur industrial corridor.
  - Deploy continuous counter-current steep auger (IMPREG-202), two-stage fluid bed dryer (DRY-501), automated optical sorter (SORT-701), and nanofiltration water recovery (RECYCLE-901).
  - Scale commercial tolling operations to achieve Year 1 throughput of 2,700 MT (60% capacity utilization), expanding to 4,500 MT (100% capacity) by Year 4.
  - Package modular 5 TPD bolt-on skids for regional mill licensing across Gujarat (Vasad), Karnataka (Gulbarga), and Madhya Pradesh (Indore).

  - *WP 4.1:* Industrial site acquisition, civil works, and sanitary flooring.

  - *WP 4.3:* Plant commissioning, trial runs, and FSSAI Central License acquisition.

## 4. Failsafe Stopping Rules & Risk Governance

If at any stage the following irreversible failure conditions are triggered, execution is paused immediately:

1. **Unresolvable Aftertaste Rule:** If after Phase 1 formulation screening, no wash protocol reduces surface salts below gustatory detection ($80\text{ ppm}$) or the 3-AFC panel maintains $p \le 0.05$, the project does not proceed to pilot scale.
2. **Yield Collapse Rule:** If total processing solids loss exceeds $5.0\%$ (dry matter basis) or broken generation exceeds $3.0\%$ despite two-stage drying, pilot engineering stops until mechanics are solved.
3. **Regulatory Non-Compliance Rule:** If internal phosphate residue exceeds $500\text{ mg/kg}$ ($10\%$ of FSSAI MRL) or if chemical leaching produces wastewater exceeding State Pollution Control Board COD/BOD discharge limits without feasible recovery, the process is redesigned before field deployment.
