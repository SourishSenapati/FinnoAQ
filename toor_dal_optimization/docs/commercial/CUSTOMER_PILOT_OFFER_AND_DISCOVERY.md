## 1. CUSTOMER DISCOVERY INTERVIEW QUESTIONNAIRE

This structured questionnaire is designed for in-person interviews with dal mill owners, chief operating officers, and procurement heads during Phase 0 Customer Discovery.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               CUSTOMER DISCOVERY QUESTIONNAIRE STRUCTURE               │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Section 1: Raw Material Sourcing & Price Spreads (Q1 - Q4)             │
│ Section 2: Hard-to-Cook Operational Pain & Quality Defects (Q5 - Q8)   │
│ Section 3: Existing Workarounds & Processing Economics (Q9 - Q11)       │
│ Section 4: Commercial Terms & Willingness-to-Pay for Tolling (Q12 - Q15)│
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Section 1: Raw Material Sourcing & Price Spreads
* **Q1:** What percentage of your annual milling throughput consists of imported pigeon pea (Tanzania, Mozambique, Malawi, Myanmar) versus domestic Indian crop (Latur, Gulbarga)?
* **Q2:** Over the last three marketing years (2023–2026), what has been the average price discount (INR per quintal or INR per kg) for imported African toor dal compared to Grade-A domestic desi dal in your primary mandi?
* **Q3:** At what price discount does imported dal become attractive for you to procure, despite known cooking defects?
* **Q4:** What are your primary delivery ports (Mumbai/Nhava Sheva, Chennai) and typical warehouse storage durations before milling?

### Section 2: Hard-to-Cook Operational Pain & Quality Defects
* **Q5:** When you mill imported African toor dal, what is the typical atmospheric cooking time or pressure-cooker whistle count reported by your institutional and wholesale buyers?
* **Q6:** What percentage of your finished batches from imported lots face buyer complaints, price renegotiations, or product returns due to hard texture, grittiness, or prolonged cooking?
* **Q7:** How does the hardness of imported dal affect your dehulling and splitting yield (chuni/khanda/fines percentage) compared to domestic crop?
* **Q8:** Are there specific origins (e.g., Nacala vs. Arusha vs. Malawi) or harvest years that exhibit unmanageable hardness that you refuse to buy?

### Section 3: Existing Workarounds & Processing Economics
* **Q9:** Do you currently practice blending imported dal with domestic Indian dal? If so, at what ratio (e.g., 70:30, 50:50), and what feedback do you receive regarding uneven cooking?
* **Q10:** Have you ever experimented with chemical soaking agents (such as sodium bicarbonate, baking soda, or polyphosphates)? If you stopped, what caused you to abandon the practice (aftertaste, gray discoloration, regulatory concerns, or customer rejection)?
* **Q11:** What is your current total milling operating cost per kilogram (cleaning, conditioning, dehulling, splitting, polishing, and bagging), excluding raw material costs?

### Section 4: Commercial Terms & Willingness-to-Pay
* **Q12:** If FinnoaQ can guarantee that your imported African dal will cook to complete tenderness in **two pressure-cooker whistles** with **zero chemical aftertaste** and identical visual appearance to domestic dal, what processing fee (INR/kg) would you consider fair value?
* **Q13:** Between the following two business arrangements, which would your management prefer?
  * *Option A (Tolling Service):* You deliver imported dal to a nearby FinnoaQ conditioning facility, pay a fixed processing fee of ₹12/kg, and receive upgraded 2-whistle dal ready for sale.
  * *Option B (Turnkey Plant Purchase):* You invest ₹135 Lakhs to install a FinnoaQ conditioning line inside your mill, pay a ₹1.50/kg royalty, and operate the line yourself.
* **Q14:** Would you be willing to provide a 5-kg representative sample of your most challenging imported HTC lot for a complimentary laboratory diagnostic and 2-whistle demonstration?
* **Q15:** If the laboratory bench test successfully passes your quality inspection, would your mill be prepared to host a 1 TPD pilot demonstration trial?

## 2. STANDARDIZED 5-KG SAMPLE INGESTION & DIAGNOSTIC PROTOCOL

To ensure technical integrity and avoid disputes over sample authenticity, all customer samples submitted for laboratory screening must follow this standardized protocol:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 SAMPLE INGESTION & DIAGNOSTIC WORKFLOW                 │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Custody Intake & Barcoding (Lot Origin, Harvest Year, Mill ID)     │
│ 2. Physical Quality Inspection (Moisture, Brokens, Foreign Matter)     │
│ 3. Baseline Cooking & Texture Assay (Hawkins Cooker + TA.XTPlus)       │
│ 4. Biochemical Screening (Pectin DE, Free Ca²⁺/Mg²⁺, Phytate)          │
│ 5. S1-09 Adaptive Bench Conditioning (24 min at 48°C)                  │
│ 6. Verification Cook & 3-AFC Sensory Tasting With Client               │
│ 7. Formal Diagnostic Report Issuance (7 Business Days)                │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2.1 Sample Reception & Chain of Custody
1. **Quantity Required:** Exactly $5.0\text{ kg} \pm 0.1\text{ kg}$ of dehulled split pigeon pea (toor dal).
2. **Packaging:** Shipped in a hermetically sealed, double-layered polyethylene bag (minimum thickness $100\ \mu\text{m}$) inside a corrugated outer carton to prevent moisture uptake or contamination during transit.
3. **Mandatory Custody Metadata:** The package must contain a signed Chain-of-Custody slip indicating:
   * Client Name, Mill Location, and Contact Person.
   * Lot Trade Name and Origin (e.g., "Tanzania Arusha Split").
   * Estimated Harvest Year and Port Ingestion Date.
   * Storage Duration and Warehouse Location.
   * Current Mandi Purchase Price (₹/kg).

### 2.2 Physical Inspection & Defect Baseline
Upon receipt at FinnoaQ’s testing laboratory, the sample is cataloged with a unique tracking code (`SMPL-YYYYMMDD-XXX`) and subjected to baseline physical grading:
* **Initial Moisture Content:** AOAC 925.10 (forced air oven at 105°C, $n=3$). Target: $10.0\%\text{--}14.0\%$.
* **Size Distribution:** Sieve analysis via Ro-Tap shaker (screens: $> 5.0\text{ mm}$, $4.0\text{--}5.0\text{ mm}$, $< 4.0\text{ mm}$).
* **Broken & Defective Grains:** Manual separation per IS 4333 (Part 1). Samples with $> 5.0\%$ broken dal require pre-screening.
* **Baseline Cooking Hardness ($F_{\mathrm{WB},0}$):** 200 g dal cooked in 600 mL water inside a calibrated Hawkins 3.0 L cooker for 2 whistles ($105\text{ kPa}$). Shear hardness measured across 20 individual cotyledons using a TA.XTPlus texture analyzer equipped with a Warner-Bratzler shear cell.

### 2.3 Diagnostic Deliverable: Client Evaluation Dossier
Within 7 business days of sample intake, FinnoaQ delivers a formal **Diagnostic & Treatability Report** to the client containing:
1. Certified baseline cooking hardness curve (shear force vs. whistle count).
2. Pectin demethoxylation index and divalent cation crosslinking density.
3. Post-treatment 2-whistle cooking proof ($F_{\mathrm{WB}} \le 18.0\text{ N}$).
4. Side-by-side photograph of raw and cooked grain vs. domestic desi dal.
5. Customized commercial proposal indicating exact tolling savings or turnkey line payback period for that specific feedstock lot.

## 3. COMMERCIAL PILOT AGREEMENT TERM SHEET (1 TPD DEMONSTRATION PLANT)

This binding Term Sheet defines the operational, financial, and legal framework governing Phase 3 commercial pilot deployment between FinnoaQ and a qualified host mill partner.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 COMMERCIAL PILOT AGREEMENT SUMMARY                     │
├───────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Host Mill Partner     │ [Partner Dal Mill Name], Latur/Akola           │
│ Technology Provider   │ FinnoaQ Agro-Industrial Technologies Ltd.      │
│ Demonstration Scale   │ 1.0 Metric Tonne per Day (1 TPD) Mobile Line   │
│ Trial Duration        │ 30 Operating Days (Total 25 MT Throughput)     │
│ Core Objective        │ Demonstrate 2-Whistle Cookability & Zero Taste │
│ Performance Guarantee │ Hawkins Cooker F_WB ≤ 18 N; 100% Fee Refund    │
└───────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 3.1 Scope of the Demonstration
1. **Pilot Plant Installation:** FinnoaQ will install and commission a skid-mounted 1 TPD automated pulse conditioning line inside the Host Mill's facility.
2. **Operational Scope:** The line will process a total of **25 metric tonnes of imported hard-to-cook toor dal** over a 30-day operating window.
3. **Product Target:** Upgraded dal must meet the **FinnoaQ 2-Whistle Specification**:
   * Complete cooked tenderness in $\le 2$ pressure-cooker whistles ($F_{\mathrm{WB}} \le 18.0\text{ N}$).
   * Zero statistically significant chemical aftertaste ($p > 0.05$ against domestic control).
   * Finished grain moisture $10.5\% \pm 0.5\%$; water activity $a_w \le 0.58$.
   * Broken grain increase $< 1.5\%$; grain checking rate $< 2.0\%$.

### 3.2 Obligations of the Parties
* **Obligations of FinnoaQ:**
  1. Deliver, install, and commission the 1 TPD pilot skid at its own expense.
  2. Supply all proprietary S1-09 chemical buffer reagents required for the 25 MT trial.
  3. Provide full-time chemical and process engineering supervision (led by Lead Engineer Sourish Senapati or designated staff).
  4. Perform daily on-site laboratory testing (moisture, texture, sensory, and residue logging).
  5. Bear full liability for pilot skid equipment maintenance and repair.
* **Obligations of the Host Mill Partner:**
  1. Provide approximately $1,500\text{ sq ft}$ of covered industrial floor space with adequate drainage.
  2. Provide utility hookups: low-pressure saturated steam ($3.5\text{ bar}$, approx. $50\text{ kg/hr}$), three-phase power ($415\text{ V}$, $15\text{ kW}$ connected), and potable water ($1,500\text{ L/day}$).
  3. Supply 25 metric tonnes of genuine imported hard-to-cook split toor dal.
  4. Assign 2 plant operators to participate in training and routine machine operation.
  5. Package and distribute the conditioned dal through its established commercial wholesale channels.

### 3.3 Commercial Terms & Pricing Structure
* **Fixed Demonstration Fee:** The Host Mill agrees to pay a subsidized demonstration processing fee of **₹8.00 per kg raw dal** processed during the 25 MT trial (Total: **₹2,00,000**), payable in two tranches:
  * Tranche 1 (₹1,00,000): Upon mechanical completion and water-testing sign-off.
  * Tranche 2 (₹1,00,000): Upon completion of the 25 MT run and delivery of the final validation dossier.
* **Commercial Purchase Option (Post-Trial):**
  * The Host Mill is granted an exclusive 60-day option to either:
    * *Option 1 (Turnkey Purchase):* Purchase a dedicated 15 TPD turnkey line with a **100% credit of the ₹2,00,000 demonstration fee** against the purchase price.
    * *Option 2 (Long-Term Tolling):* Execute a 3-year commercial tolling contract at ₹12.00/kg with guaranteed minimum monthly volume of 150 MT.

### 3.4 Uncompromised Performance Guarantee & Remedies
* **The 2-Whistle Guarantee:** If any conditioned batch of dal processed during the trial fails to achieve cooked tenderness ($F_{\mathrm{WB}} \le 18.0\text{ N}$) after 2 whistles in a Hawkins 3.0 L cooker, or exhibits detectable chemical aftertaste in a certified 3-AFC panel:
  1. **Immediate Remedy:** FinnoaQ will re-process the batch at its own expense to meet specification.
  2. **Fee Forfeiture:** If the re-processed batch still fails specification, the Host Mill is completely exempt from paying the tolling fee for that batch.
  3. **Full Refund:** If $> 15\%$ of total trial volume fails specification, FinnoaQ will immediately refund 100% of the demonstration fee paid to date.
  4. **Feedstock Indemnity:** If equipment malfunction causes physical damage or contamination rendering the grain unmarketable, FinnoaQ will reimburse the Host Mill for the raw material cost at prevailing mandi spot rates.

### 3.5 Confidentiality & Intellectual Property
* All intellectual property, process flowsheets, chemical formulations, software algorithms, and equipment designs remain the sole and exclusive property of **FinnoaQ Agro-Industrial Technologies Ltd.**
* The Host Mill agrees not to reverse-engineer, sample, chemically analyze, or disclose the composition of the S1-09 buffer or equipment internal geometry to third parties for a period of 5 years.
