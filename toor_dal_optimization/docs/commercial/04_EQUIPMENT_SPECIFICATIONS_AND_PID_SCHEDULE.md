## 1. Process Flow Architecture & Unit Operations

The 15 TPD industrial processing facility is designed as a continuous, automated production line engineered to minimize mechanical impact and preserve whole split-cotyledon integrity.

```
[Raw HTC Dal Infeed] ──> [Pre-Cleaner & Destoner] ──> [Chemical Dosing / Make-Up]
                                                                │
                                                                ▼
[Fluid Bed Zone 1] <── [Counter-Current Rinse] <── [Dewatering] <── [Steep Auger Reactor]
        │
        ▼
[Tempering Chamber] ──> [Fluid Bed Zone 2] ──> [Fluidized Cooler] ──> [Optical Sorter] ──> [Packaging]
```

## 3. Comprehensive Utilities & Energy Schedule

The 15 TPD facility operates continuously for 24 hours per day (three 8-hour shifts), processing 625 kg/h of dry dal feed:

### 3.1 Electrical Power Distribution
- **Total Connected Load:** **$78.6\text{ kW}$**
- **Operating Load Factor:** $0.69$ (diversity and variable-speed operation)
- **Running Electrical Demand:** **$54.2\text{ kW}$**
- **Daily Electrical Consumption:** $54.2\text{ kW} \times 24\text{ h} = \mathbf{1,300.8\text{ kWh / day}}$
- **Specific Power Consumption:** $1,300.8 / 14.36\text{ MT} = \mathbf{90.59\text{ kWh / MT finished product}}$
- **Power Cost per Kilogram:** At industrial tariff of ₹8.00/kWh (MSEDCL Industrial Tariff HT-1), power cost is **₹0.725 / kg** (allocated as ₹1.82/kg across all auxiliary drives, air compressors, and chillers).

### 3.2 Thermal Energy & Steam Generation
- **Total Thermal Load:**
  - *Steep Preheating (48°C):* $18.4\text{ kW}$
  - *Dryer Zone 1 (62°C):* $85.0\text{ kW}$
  - *Dryer Zone 2 (52°C):* $42.4\text{ kW}$
  - *Total Continuous Thermal Duty:* **$145.8\text{ kW}$** ($125,400\text{ kcal/h}$)
- **Steam Requirement:** Saturated steam at $3.0\text{ bar gauge}$ ($143.6^\circ\text{C}$, latent heat $h_{fg} = 2,133\text{ kJ/kg}$):
  $$\dot{m}_{\text{steam}} = \frac{145.8\text{ kW} \times 3,600\text{ s/h}}{2,133\text{ kJ/kg}} = \mathbf{246.0\text{ kg steam / h}}$$
- **Boiler Selection:** A $300\text{ kg/h}$ multi-fuel package steam boiler operating on agricultural biomass briquettes (groundnut shell / mustard husk briquettes, calorific value $4,000\text{ kcal/kg}$ at ₹4,500/MT).
- **Boiler Operating Cost:** Biomass consumption is $34.5\text{ kg/h}$, generating thermal steam cost of **₹1.15 / kg finished dal**.

### 3.3 Water Balance & Nanofiltration Recovery
- **Raw Rinse Water Demand:** $0.50\text{ L/kg} \times 15,000\text{ kg/day} = \mathbf{7,500\text{ L / day}}$ ($7.50\text{ m}^3/\text{day}$).
- **Nanofiltration Permeate Recycle:** $85\%$ permeate recovery recovers **$6,375\text{ L / day}$** of purified water back to the Stage 1 wash tank.
- **Net Fresh Makeup Water:** **$1,125\text{ L / day}$** ($1.13\text{ m}^3/\text{day}$).
- **Concentrated Brine Effluent:** **$1,125\text{ L / day}$** containing concentrated sodium citrate and SHMP. The brine passes through a small solar evaporation pan or mechanical vapor recompression unit to yield a solid mineral fertilizer by-product (NPK value from phosphate and citrate), producing **zero liquid discharge (ZLD)**.

## 4. Instrumentation, Sensors & Industrial Control Architecture (P&ID)

The plant utilizes a centralized **Siemens S7-1200 PLC** linked to an industrial touch-panel HMI and SCADA logging system to guarantee batch-to-batch repeatability and prevent out-of-spec product.

```
[Level / Mass Sensors] ──> [Siemens S7-1200 PLC] <── [NIR In-Line Moisture]
                                  │
          ┌───────────────────────┼───────────────────────┐
          ▼                       ▼                       ▼
[Variable Speed Drives]  [Modulating Steam Valves]  [Conductivity Cut-Off]
```

### Critical Instrumentation Points
1. **In-Line NIR Moisture Probes (NIR-101 & NIR-102):**
   - Mounted at the dewatering screen exit (target $28.5 \pm 0.5\%$) and fluid-bed discharge (target $10.5 \pm 0.3\%$). Continuously modulates fluid-bed bed vibration speed and burner steam supply to prevent over-drying or moisture pockets.
2. **Sanitary RTD Pt100 Sensors (TT-201 to TT-205):**
   - 4-wire RTDs (Class A, accuracy $\pm 0.15^\circ\text{C}$) installed in steep auger, dryer plenum, tempering chamber, and cooler discharge.
3. **Electromagnetic Flow Meters (FT-201 & FT-401):**
   - Magnetic flow transmitters on S1-09 buffer dosing line and counter-current wash water line to enforce exact $L/S = 1.60$ and wash ratio $0.50\text{ L/kg}$.
4. **Effluent In-Line Conductivity Monitor (CT-401):**
   - Continuously measures rinse drain conductivity. If conductivity exceeds $120\text{ }\mu\text{S/cm}$ (indicating incomplete surface salt clearance), an automated three-way diverter valve recycles the dal for a secondary 10-second rinse before entering Dryer Zone 1.
