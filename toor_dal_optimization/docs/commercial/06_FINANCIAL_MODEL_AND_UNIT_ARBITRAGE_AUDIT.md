## 1. Commercial Arbitrage Opportunity & Unit Economics

The FinnoaQ commercial value proposition is anchored in the structural price disparity between imported hard-to-cook (HTC) pigeon pea and domestic desi dal across major Indian mandis (Latur, Akola, Gulbarga, Indore).

### 1.1 The ₹20.00/kg Mandi Spread
- **Domestic Desi Toor Dal (Latur Maruti Grade):** Mandi trading price: **₹125.00 to ₹145.00 / kg** (Baseline: **₹132.00 / kg**).
- **Imported African Pigeon Pea (Tanzania / Mozambique):** Mandi trading price: **₹102.00 to ₹125.00 / kg** (Baseline: **₹112.00 / kg**).
- **Structural Market Discount:** **₹18.00 to ₹24.00 / kg** (Audited baseline: **₹20.00 / kg**).

### 1.2 Profit Distribution Under the B2B Tolling Model
FinnoaQ operates as a specialized conditioning processor, charging a processing tolling fee of **₹12.00 / kg** to pulse millers.

$$\begin{array}{lrr}
\hline
\textbf{Commercial Metric} & \textbf{Value (₹/kg)} & \textbf{Value (₹/MT)} \\
\hline
\text{Raw African Mandi Discount vs. Desi} & ₹20.00 & ₹20,000 \\
\text{FinnoaQ Tolling Fee Charged to Miller} & (₹12.00) & (₹12,000) \\
\hline
\textbf{Net Profit Lift Captured by Miller} & \mathbf{₹8.00} & \mathbf{₹8,000} \\
\hline
\text{FinnoaQ Total Direct Operating Cost (OpEx)} & (₹6.34) & (₹6,340) \\
\hline
\textbf{FinnoaQ Gross Operating Profit (EBITDA)} & \mathbf{₹5.66} & \mathbf{₹5,660} \\
\textbf{FinnoaQ Gross Operating Margin (\%)} & \mathbf{47.17\%} & \mathbf{47.17\%} \\
\hline
\end{array}$$

**The Miller's Value Proposition:** By paying a ₹12.00/kg tolling fee, the mill owner upgrades ₹112/kg imported stock into a ₹132/kg finished 2-whistle equivalent dal. The miller captures **₹8,000 per metric tonne in pure net profit** with zero capital investment, zero technology risk, and no operational disruption to their primary milling line.

## 4. Investment Return Metrics & Breakeven Analysis

### 4.1 Return Metrics
- **Turnkey Capital Investment:** **₹135.00 Lakhs** (plus ₹15.00L initial working capital = ₹150.00L total Year 0 outlay).
- **Internal Rate of Return (5-Year IRR):** **$78.4\%$**
- **Net Present Value (NPV @ 12.0% Discount Rate):** **₹642.15 Lakhs**
- **Discounted Payback Period:** **1.12 Years** (13.4 months after plant commissioning).
- **Return on Capital Employed (ROCE at Steady State):** **$158.8\%$**.

### 4.2 Cash Break-Even Analysis
Fixed annual cash expenses (Labour ₹36.6L + Maintenance ₹5.4L + Debt Service ₹29.5L + SG&A ₹12.0L) total **₹83.50 Lakhs per year**.
- Net variable contribution margin: **₹7.86 / kg finished dal** (Tolling fee ₹12.00 minus variable utilities/chemicals ₹4.14).
- **Cash Break-Even Volume:**
  $$\text{Volume}_{\text{BE}} = \frac{₹83,50,000}{₹7.86 / \text{kg}} = 1,062,341\text{ kg} = \mathbf{1,062.3\text{ MT / year}}$$
- **Break-Even Utilization:** $1,062.3 / 4,500 = \mathbf{23.6\%}$ (Under zero-debt equity structure: **$18.7\%$**).
The plant requires only **2.80 TPD** of finished throughput to cover all operating expenses.

## 5. Multi-Variable Sensitivity Analysis Matrix

A two-dimensional stress test was conducted evaluating variations in Tolling Fee (₹8.00 to ₹14.00/kg) against Plant Capacity Utilization (40% to 100%):

### Annual Operating EBITDA Matrix (₹ in Lakhs)

| Tolling Fee (₹/kg) | 40% Utilization (1,800 MT) | 60% Utilization (2,700 MT) | 80% Utilization (3,600 MT) | 100% Utilization (4,500 MT) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **₹8.00 / kg** | ₹29.88 L | ₹44.82 L | ₹59.76 L | ₹74.70 L |
| **₹10.00 / kg** | ₹65.88 L | ₹98.82 L | ₹131.76 L | ₹164.70 L |
| **₹12.00 / kg (Baseline)**| **₹101.88 L** | **₹127.82 L** | **₹178.76 L** | **₹224.70 L** |
| **₹14.00 / kg** | ₹137.88 L | ₹170.82 L | ₹225.76 L | ₹284.70 L |

### Downside Stress-Test Scenarios
1. **Severe Mandi Discount Compression:** If imported African dal discount shrinks from ₹20.00/kg to ₹12.00/kg, FinnoaQ lowers tolling fee to ₹8.00/kg (leaving ₹4.00/kg to miller). At 80% capacity, EBITDA remains strongly positive at **₹59.76 Lakhs/year**; IRR remains healthy at **$28.2\%$**.
2. **20% CapEx Overrun (CapEx increases to ₹162.00 Lakhs):** Payback period extends slightly from 1.12 years to **1.34 years**; 5-year IRR remains outstanding at **$66.5\%$**.
3. **Power Tariff Spike (+25% to ₹10.00/kWh):** OpEx increases by ₹0.18/kg. EBITDA drops by ₹8.10 Lakhs/year ($3.6\%$ impact), fully absorbed by operating margins.
