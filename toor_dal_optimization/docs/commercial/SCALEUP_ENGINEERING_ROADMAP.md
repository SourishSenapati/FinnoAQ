## 1. SCALE-UP PHILOSOPHY & GOVERNANCE RULES

FinnoaQ operates under a strict **staged-gate engineering governance model**. Scaling a food-processing chemical intervention without empirical verification carries catastrophic risks: unpalatable aftertaste, grain cracking, uneven treatment, or unmanageable wastewater burdens.

To protect investor capital and partner relationships:
1. **Zero Premature Scale-Up:** Equipment sizing and fabrication commitments for Phase $(N+1)$ are strictly forbidden until Phase $N$ exit criteria have been independently audited and signed off.
2. **Hard Spending Caps:** Every phase operates under an inviolable maximum budget ceiling. Unforeseen technical obstacles trigger a formal pause and diagnostic audit rather than capital overruns.
3. **Formal Stopping Rules:** If a fundamental thermodynamic, sensory, or regulatory barrier is encountered that cannot be resolved within budget, the project is halted and capital returned.
4. **No Commercial Performance Guarantees in Early Phases:** Customer-facing agreements in Phases 0 and 1 explicitly state that the process is under diagnostic and bench development; no performance guarantees are issued until Gate 2 is unlocked.

## 2. PHASE-BY-PHASE DETAILED EXECUTION PLAN

### 2.1 PHASE 0: Evidence and Customer Discovery
* **Duration:** Months 1–2 (8 Weeks)
* **Maximum Spend Ceiling:** **₹12.00 Lakhs**
* **Operating Scale:** Analytical laboratory benchtop (100 g to 1.0 kg samples).
* **Core Objective:** Establish empirical baselines on genuine imported grain, eliminate unverified model claims, and interview commercial dal millers to solidify product requirements.

#### Work Packages (Phase 0)
1. **WP 0.1: Feedstock Procurement & Chain-of-Custody:** Secure five authentic 10-kg commercial bags of pigeon pea representing the major trade flows in Maharashtra mandis:
   * Sample 01: Tanzanian Arusha crop (harvested 2024/2025; stored $> 6$ months).
   * Sample 02: Mozambican Nacala crop (high-humidity coastal transit).
   * Sample 03: Malawian imported dal.
   * Sample 04: Myanmar Lemon / White toor dal.
   * Sample 05: Fresh domestic Indian desi toor dal (Latur/Gulbarga benchmark control).
2. **WP 0.2: Baseline Analytical Profiling:** Measure true physicochemical properties across all 5 samples:
   * Moisture content (AOAC 925.10 vacuum oven).
   * Pectin degree of esterification (FTIR 1740/1630 $\text{cm}^{-1}$ peak ratio).
   * Free vs. bound $\mathrm{Ca}^{2+}$ and $\mathrm{Mg}^{2+}$ (microwave acid digestion followed by ICP-OES).
   * Phytic acid content (Wade reagent colorimetry).
   * Raw grain hardness and initial 2-whistle cooking hardness ($F_{\mathrm{WB}}$ via TA.XTPlus texture analyzer).
3. **WP 0.3: Customer Discovery Interviews (Latur & Akola):** Conduct 15 structured in-person interviews with dal mill owners, plant managers, and pulse brokers:
   * Quantify historical price discounts on African lots over the last 3 years.
   * Document existing workarounds, failure rates, and customer complaint logs.
   * Test commercial terms: tolling fee willingness-to-pay (₹10 vs. ₹12 vs. ₹15/kg) and interest in pilot trials.

#### Phase 0 Decision Gates & Criteria
* **Entry Gate 0:** Receipt of certified seed capital (₹12 Lakhs); formal engagement of Lead Chemical and Process Engineer Sourish Senapati.
* **Exit Gate 0 (Go/No-Go):**
  1. Empirical confirmation that untreated imported Tanzanian/Mozambican lots exhibit cooked shear force $F_{\mathrm{WB}} \ge 32.0\text{ N}$ after 2 whistles (proving the existence of the commercial HTC defect).
  2. Execution of at least **3 non-binding Letters of Intent (LOIs)** with commercial dal millers agreeing to supply 1 TPD of dal for Phase 3 demonstration trials.
* **Hard Stopping Rule 0:** If imported African lots show no significant cooking deficit compared to domestic dal ($F_{\mathrm{WB}} \le 20.0\text{ N}$ naturally), the commercial premise is invalid; halt project and return remaining capital.

### 2.2 PHASE 1: Controlled Bench Validation
* **Duration:** Months 3–5 (12 Weeks)
* **Maximum Spend Ceiling:** **₹33.00 Lakhs**
* **Operating Scale:** Controlled bench batches (1.0 kg to 5.0 kg).
* **Core Objective:** Prove under controlled conditions that S1-09 chelation conditioning achieves 2-whistle tenderness, zero chemical aftertaste, and FSSAI regulatory compliance.

#### Work Packages (Phase 1)
1. **WP 1.1: Chelation Boundary-Layer Optimization:** Run 20 factorial bench trials optimizing:
   * Soak duration: 15, 20, 24, 30, and 40 minutes.
   * Soak temperature: 35°C, 42°C, 48°C, and 55°C.
   * Buffer formulation: S1-09 (citrate + citric + SHMP) vs. binary controls (citrate alone, phosphate alone).
2. **WP 1.2: Standardized 2-Whistle Pressure Cooking Assay:**
   * Cook 200 g conditioned dal in 600 mL water inside a calibrated Hawkins 3.0 L pressure cooker operating at $105\text{ kPa}$ gauge pressure.
   * Record exact time to first whistle, time between whistles, total cook time, and natural depressurization cooling curve.
   * Measure Warner-Bratzler shear force ($n=20$ grains per trial) using TA.XTPlus texture analyzer.
3. **WP 1.3: Sensory Aftertaste & Flavor Panel:**
   * Double-blind 3-AFC (Alternative Forced Choice) triangle test with a trained 12-member sensory panel.
   * Panelists must differentiate between: (A) Treated HTC dal, (B) Untreated domestic Indian toor dal, and (C) Domestic dal treated with identical buffer.
   * Statistically verify that aftertaste difference is non-significant ($p > 0.05$).
4. **WP 1.4: Chemical Residue & Wash Efficiency:**
   * Evaluate rinse liquid-to-solids ratio ($0.25$, $0.50$, $1.0\text{ L/kg}$) in removing surface salts.
   * Quantify surface and core residual sodium and phosphate via ICP-OES.
   * Verify adherence to FSSAI Regulation 2.4.6 and Table 1 food additive limits.

#### Phase 1 Decision Gates & Criteria
* **Entry Gate 1:** Passage of Exit Gate 0; installation of benchtop agitator bath, Hawkins calibrated cooker, and texture analyzer cell.
* **Exit Gate 1 (Go/No-Go):**
  1. Cooked shear force $F_{\mathrm{WB}} \le 18.0\text{ N}$ achieved on $\ge 5$ consecutive 5-kg batches of Tanzanian HTC dal under the 2-whistle protocol.
  2. Sensory triangle panel confirms zero statistically significant chemical aftertaste ($p > 0.05$ against domestic control).
  3. Total finished phosphate residue verified $< 500\text{ mg/kg}$ ($< 10\%$ of FSSAI 5,000 mg/kg MRL).
  4. Total solids loss during conditioning and washing verified $\le 3.5\%$.
* **Hard Stopping Rule 1:** If 2-whistle tenderness cannot be reached without exceeding sensory taste thresholds ($OAV \ge 1.0$) or statutory phosphate limits, halt project execution.

### 2.3 PHASE 2: Engineering Pilot Skid
* **Duration:** Months 6–9 (16 Weeks)
* **Maximum Spend Ceiling:** **₹65.00 Lakhs**
* **Operating Scale:** Pilot batch skid (25 kg to 100 kg/batch; 500 kg/day).
* **Core Objective:** Design, fabricate, and operate a pilot conditioning and drying skid to validate fluid-bed heat transfer, eliminate grain checking cracks, and confirm unit OpEx.

#### Work Packages (Phase 2)
1. **WP 2.1: Pilot Skid Engineering & Procurement:** Procure and integrate skid-mounted units:
   * 100-kg batch-rotary soak drum with variable speed drive and hot water heating loop.
   * Continuous perforated centrifugal rinse drum ($0.50\text{ L/kg}$ spray manifold).
   * Vibratory pilot fluid-bed dryer (2.5 m length, 15 kW electric/steam air heater, variable air velocity 1.0–3.0 m/s).
   * Insulated gravity tempering silo ($0.5\text{ m}^3$).
2. **WP 2.2: Two-Stage Drying & Stress Validation:**
   * Test the two-stage drying curve: Stage 1 (62°C, 22 min) $\rightarrow$ Tempering (45°C, 20 min) $\rightarrow$ Stage 2 (52°C, 26 min).
   * Measure moisture distribution across 100 individual grains post-tempering.
   * Quantify internal crack density under darkfield stereomicroscopy. Target: checking rate $< 2.0\%$, brokens $< 1.5\%$.
3. **WP 2.3: Pilot OpEx & Energy Audit:**
   * Measure real electrical kWh draw and thermal steam consumption per kg of dal processed.
   * Track chemical reagent recovery and wash-water recycling in a pilot settling tank.
   * Confirm that normalized production OpEx does not exceed ₹7.50/kg at pilot scale.

#### Phase 2 Decision Gates & Criteria
* **Entry Gate 2:** Passage of Exit Gate 1; completion of Front-End Engineering Design (FEED) for the pilot skid.
* **Exit Gate 2 (Go/No-Go):**
  1. Continuous processing of 500 kg/day across 10 operating days with zero mechanical breakdown.
  2. Grain checking / internal crack rate verified $< 2.0\%$ and broken grains $< 1.5\%$.
  3. Water activity of dried product verified $a_w \le 0.58$ at $10.5\% \pm 0.5\%$ moisture.
  4. Pilot OpEx verified $\le ₹7.50\text{ per kg raw dal}$.
* **Hard Stopping Rule 2:** If fluid-bed drying causes $> 5.0\%$ broken dal or unmanageable grain checking that cannot be resolved by tempering, halt further scale-up until dryer geometry is redesigned.

### 2.4 PHASE 3: Demonstration Plant (Partner Mill Deployment)
* **Duration:** Months 10–14 (20 Weeks)
* **Maximum Spend Ceiling:** **₹120.00 Lakhs**
* **Operating Scale:** 1.0 Metric Tonne per Day (1 TPD) continuous demonstration line.
* **Core Objective:** Install and operate a 1 TPD demonstration line inside an operating commercial dal mill in Latur under realistic industrial factory conditions.

#### Work Packages (Phase 3)
1. **WP 3.1: Host Mill Site Integration:** Finalize partnership agreement with an anchor dal mill in Latur MIDC (providing 1,500 sq ft floor space, 3.5 bar steam hookup, 40 kW electrical drop, and drain trench).
2. **WP 3.2: 1 TPD Demonstration Line Installation:** Install skid-mounted 1 TPD system:
   * Automated raw dal feeding hopper with load cells.
   * Continuous rotary conditioning cylinder (`SKR-102` prototype).
   * Counter-current wash drum (`WSH-103`).
   * Continuous two-stage fluid-bed dryer with integral tempering zone (`DRY-104`).
   * Fluid-bed ambient cooler and bagging scale.
3. **WP 3.3: 30-Day Continuous Commercial Trial:**
   * Run the line for 30 consecutive operating days (20 hr/day; 1,000 kg/day output).
   * Condition 25 MT of imported Tanzanian and Mozambican dal.
   * Train host mill operators to manage buffer preparation, CIP cleaning cycles, and dryer tuning.
4. **WP 3.4: Commercial Market Distribution:**
   * Package conditioned dal in commercial 30-kg bags under partner mill's standard packaging.
   * Distribute 25 MT across wholesale mandis in Mumbai, Pune, and Hyderabad.
   * Collect wholesale trade feedback, retail customer acceptance, and monitor secondary cooking complaints.

#### Phase 3 Decision Gates & Criteria
* **Entry Gate 3:** Passage of Exit Gate 2; execution of formal Demonstration Host Mill Agreement with defined utility sharing and liability caps.
* **Exit Gate 3 (Go/No-Go):**
  1. Successful completion of 30-day continuous trial producing $\ge 25\text{ MT}$ of conditioned dal.
  2. Zero customer complaints regarding cooking time or aftertaste from wholesale distribution.
  3. Audited commercial production OpEx verified $\le ₹6.80\text{ per kg}$.
  4. Host mill or consortium exercises option to license technology (Model C) or signs long-term tolling contract (Model D).
* **Hard Stopping Rule 3:** If commercial wholesale buyers reject the dal due to cooking inconsistency or perceived texture abnormalities, halt Phase 4 commercial deployment.

### 2.5 PHASE 4: Full Commercial Scale (5 TPD Modular / 15 TPD Industrial Line)
* **Duration:** Months 15–24 (40 Weeks)
* **Maximum Capital Deployment:** **₹350.00 Lakhs** (funded via Project Debt, Commercial Tolling Cash Flows, and Series A Equity).
* **Operating Scale:** 5 TPD modular line expanding to 15 TPD full industrial line (4,500 MT/year).
* **Core Objective:** Commercial rollout of FinnoaQ’s flagship 15 TPD industrial tolling facility in Latur MIDC.

#### Work Packages (Phase 4)
1. **WP 4.1: Industrial Facility EPC:** Procure, erect, and commission the complete 10-unit operation schedule (`CLN-101` to `PKG-110`) detailed in Section 6 of Document A.
2. **WP 4.2: Full SCADA / PLC Automation:** Integrate Siemens S7-1200 PLC automation with recipe management, automated chemical dosing, psychrometric drying air modulation, and cloud-linked IoT telemetry.
3. **WP 4.3: Commercial Tolling Ramp-Up:** Execute binding tolling agreements with 4 anchor dal millers in Latur, ramping plant utilization from 60% in Month 16 to 100% (4,500 MT/year) by Month 24.
4. **WP 4.4: Commercial Performance:** Achieve monthly EBITDA $> ₹15.0\text{ Lakhs}$ with unlevered monthly free cash flow $> ₹12.0\text{ Lakhs}$.

## 4. RFQ PROCUREMENT CRITERIA & VENDOR SELECTION NORMS

To ensure industrial reliability and prevent cost overruns, all equipment for Phases 2, 3, and 4 must comply with formal engineering procurement specifications:

### 4.1 Fabrication & Material Standards
* **Product Contact Surfaces:** All surfaces contacting dal, buffer solutions, or rinse water must be fabricated from **AISI 304 or 316 stainless steel**, polished to a sanitary finish of $R_a \le 0.8\ \mu\text{m}$.
* **Structural Frames & Non-Contact:** Carbon steel IS 2062, sandblasted to Sa 2.5 and coated with two coats of epoxy primer and two coats of polyurethane food-grade paint.
* **Gaskets & Seals:** Food-grade EPDM or PTFE conforming to FDA 21 CFR 177.2600.
* **Motors & Drives:** TEFC squirrel cage induction motors, IP55 enclosure, Class F insulation, IE3 premium efficiency (ABB, Siemens, or Bharat Bijlee).
* **Gearboxes:** Helical or planetary gearboxes with synthetic food-grade lubrication (Bonfiglioli, Radicon, or SEW-Eurodrive).

### 4.2 Approved Vendor Shortlist

| Equipment Category | Approved Indian Vendors | Evaluation Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Vibratory Screeners & Destoners** | Milltec Machinery, Indopol, Fowler Westrup | Screen stratification efficiency $> 99\%$; dynamic balancing; SS304 screens. |
| **Soaking & Chemical Vessels** | Praj Industries, Trojan Fabricators, SSP India | ASME Section VIII Div 1 welding; sanitary internal lifters; sanitary CIP spray balls. |
| **Centrifugal Rinse Drums** | Fabcon Food Processing, Goma Engineering | Perforated drum concentricity $< 1.0\text{ mm}$; variable spray pressure 2–5 bar. |

## 5. PILOT MILL PARTNER SELECTION PROTOCOL

Selecting the right commercial mill partner for Phase 3 Demonstration is vital for market credibility. FinnoaQ enforces four strict criteria for partner qualification:

1. **Geographic Clustering:** Located in a primary pulse processing hub (Latur, Akola, or Kalaburagi) with ready access to imported African dal shipments.
2. **Throughput & Infrastructure:** Minimum mill operating capacity of $\ge 50\text{ TPD}$; existing operational steam boiler ($> 500\text{ kg/hr}$ spare capacity); 415 V three-phase industrial power; covered factory space $\ge 1,500\text{ sq ft}$.
3. **Commercial Mindset:** Progressive leadership willing to sign a formal 1-year demonstration agreement, participate in wholesale market seeding, and provide authentic historical yield and billing data.
4. **Quality Commitment:** Dedicated on-site quality control technician and certified moisture testing equipment.
