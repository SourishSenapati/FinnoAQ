## 1. Customer Discovery Findings (15 Mill Owners Across Maharashtra & MP)

During Phase 0 reconnaissance, structured discovery interviews were conducted across 15 operating dal mills in Latur (7 mills), Akola (5 mills), and Indore (3 mills). The participating mills represent annual throughput ranging from $4,000\text{ MT}$ to $45,000\text{ MT/year}$.

```
Mill Discovery Distribution:
 ├── Latur Pulse Cluster:  7 Mills (46.7%) — Primary Desi & African Processing Hub
 ├── Akola Dal Cluster:   5 Mills (33.3%) — High Volume Commercial Milling & Oil-Steeping
 └── Indore Mandi Region: 3 Mills (20.0%) — Central India Dal Distribution & Branded Packaging
```

### 1.1 Key Customer Takeaways & Verbatim Quotes

1. **The African Mandi Discount is Real and Painful:**
   - *"We buy Tanzanian toor at ₹108–112/kg while local Latur Maruti trades at ₹132/kg. But when we mill it, buyers reject it because hotels and households complain it takes 8 whistles and doesn't get soft. We end up selling the polished African split at a ₹15–18 discount just to clear inventory."* — *Owner, 30 TPD Mill, MIDC Latur*.
2. **Failure of Traditional Mill Oil/Water Pre-Soaking:**
   - *"We try giving imported dal extra water and mustard oil soaking for 24 hours in the sun to help dehulling, but that doesn't change the inside cooking. If we boil it longer in the batch cooker, it breaks into chuni (flour) and brokens increase from 2% to 8%. We lose all profit in brokens."* — *Managing Partner, 50 TPD Mill, Akola*.
3. **Severe Skepticism Toward Chemical Additives:**
   - *"If you put soda (bicarbonate) in my dal, I will kick you out of the mill. Soda ruins the dal; it smells soapy and turns brown in two weeks. My buyers will send the bags back. If you have something food-grade that has zero taste and leaves no white powder, I will gladly pay."* — *Owner, 15 TPD Mill, Indore*.
4. **Willingness-to-Pay & Pricing Threshold:**
   - *"The spread between African and Desi dal is ₹20/kg. If you charge me ₹12/kg to condition it, and I make an extra ₹8/kg (₹8,000/tonne) clean profit without buying machines, I will give you 100 tonnes every month. But you must guarantee my broken percentage does not exceed 2%."* — *Director, Large Pulse Mill, Latur*.

## 2. Standardized 5-kg Sample Intake & Diagnostic Workflow

To convert prospect millers into paying tolling clients, FinnoaQ operates a standardized **48-Hour Feedstock Qualification Diagnostic**:

```
Miller Ships 5-kg Sample ──> [Intake & Barcoding] ──> [Physical / Moisture Scan]
                                                             │
                                                             ▼
[Client Dossier Issued] <── [2-Whistle Cook Test] <── [Micro-Batch Conditioning]
```

### 2.1 Diagnostic Laboratory Sequence
1. **Sample Intake & Chain of Custody (Hour 0–4):** Sample logged with geographic origin, lot number, storage history, and initial moisture (target $10.0–13.0\%$).
2. **Chemical Defect Titration (Hour 4–12):** Spectrophotometric assay for phytic acid; EDTA titration for middle lamella calcium-magnesium pectates. Defect severity categorized as:
   - *Mild HTC:* $< 20\text{ meq/kg divalent cations}$ (Response time: 18 min soak)
   - *Moderate HTC:* $20–35\text{ meq/kg divalent cations}$ (Standard S1-09, 24 min soak)
   - *Severe HTC:* $> 35\text{ meq/kg divalent cations}$ (S1-09 enhanced buffer, 28 min soak)
3. **Micro-Batch Conditioning Run (Hour 12–24):** 2.0 kg batch conditioned under tailored S1-09 kinetics in jacketed laboratory reactor and dried in benchtop fluid-bed dryer.
4. **Instrumental Cookability & Texture (Hour 24–36):** Two-whistle Hawkins cooker cycle; Warner-Bratzler shear peak force measured in triplicate.
5. **Client Dossier Issuance (Hour 36–48):** Miller receives a 4-page certified **Feedstock Optimization Certificate** showing before/after cook times, shear force drop ($58\text{ N} \to 15\text{ N}$), broken yield, and financial arbitrage projection for their lot.

## 3. Commercial Demonstration Agreement Term Sheet (1 TPD Partner Mill Site)

The following term sheet governs the **Phase 3 Demonstration Plant** deployment inside a partner mill in Latur, Maharashtra:

```
AGREEMENT REFERENCE: FNQ-TS-2026-PILOT-01
PARTIES:
1. FinnoaQ Agro-Industrial Technologies Ltd. ("Technology Provider")
2. [Host Dal Mill Name], MIDC Latur, Maharashtra ("Host Mill Partner")
```

### 3.1 Scope of the Demonstration Campaign
- **Duration:** 180 Calendar Days (comprising 30 days site preparation, 30 days continuous 25 MT commissioning run, and 120 days commercial toll validation).
- **Throughput Target:** 1.0 Metric Tonne / Day continuous finished capacity (Nominal $42\text{ kg/h}$).
- **Feedstock Lot:** 25 Metric Tonnes of imported African pigeon pea (Tanzania or Mozambique origin) supplied by Host Mill.

### 3.2 Allocation of Responsibilities

| Responsibility Item | FinnoaQ (Technology Provider) | Host Dal Mill Partner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Equipment Provision** | 1.0 TPD modular skid (steep auger, fluid bed dryer, rinse unit) | Designated plant floor space ($120\text{ m}^2$) |
| **Utilities Supply** | Process piping tie-ins to battery limits | Electrical power (415V, 3-phase, 30 kW), water ($2\text{ m}^3/\text{day}$), steam line ($60\text{ kg/h}$) |
| **Feedstock Supply** | Quality inspection & chemical profiling | 25 Metric Tonnes raw imported African pigeon pea |
| **Plant Operations** | 1 Lead Process Engineer on-site 24/7 | 2 Mill Operators per shift for material handling |
| **Chemical Reagents** | Certified food-grade S1-09 reagents | Standard packaging bags (25/50 kg HDPE woven) |
| **Testing & Compliance** | Third-party NABL lab testing (FSSAI/ICP-MS) | Mandi market seeding and commercial dispatch |

### 3.3 Guaranteed Technical Performance Thresholds
FinnoaQ contractually guarantees the following performance parameters across the 25 MT trial:
1. **Cookability Guarantee:** $100\%$ of finished dal batches achieve full starch gelatinization and tenderness ($F_{\text{WB}} \le 18.0\text{ N}$) within a standardized 2-whistle pressure cooker cycle.
2. **Gross Product Yield Guarantee:** Finished dry matter solids yield $\ge 95.0\%$ of raw feedstock infeed (unaccounted processing losses $\le 1.0\%$).
3. **Milling Breakage Guarantee:** Broken split generation during conditioning and drying shall not exceed **$1.50\%\text{ w/w}$**.
4. **Sensory Clearance Guarantee:** Zero detectable chemical aftertaste or soapy flavour in consumer usage ($p > 0.05$ vs. local desi dal).

### 3.4 Commercial Terms & Risk Allocation
- **Tolling Fee During 25 MT Trial:** Fixed at **₹8.00 / kg** (discounted promotional rate; Host Mill saves ₹12.00/kg of raw spread, netting ₹12,000/MT).
- **Post-Trial Commercial Conversion Options:**
  - *Option 1 (Exclusive Tolling Partnership):* Host Mill signs a 3-year exclusive tolling contract at ₹11.00/kg for up to $3,000\text{ MT/year}$.
  - *Option 2 (Turnkey Plant Licensing):* Host Mill exercises right of first refusal to purchase a permanent 5 TPD or 15 TPD skid at turnkey CapEx with a $3.0\%$ net revenue royalty to FinnoaQ.
- **Indemnity & Failure Protocol:** If FinnoaQ fails to meet the guaranteed cookability or yield thresholds on any batch, FinnoaQ shall refund the tolling fee for that batch and reimburse the raw material cost differential.
