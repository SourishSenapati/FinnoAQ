## 4. Analytical Verification & Testing Protocols

To maintain audit defense, all processing batches are subjected to standardized laboratory testing protocols:

```
Batch Sampling ──> [Sample A: ICP-MS] ──────> Total Phosphorus & Heavy Metals
               ──> [Sample B: HPLC-UV] ─────> Free Citrate Quantification
               ──> [Sample C: Texture/Cook] ─> Warner-Bratzler 2-Whistle Shear Force
               ──> [Sample D: ISO 4120] ────> 3-AFC Sensory Triangle Panel
```

### 4.1 ICP-MS Protocol for Phosphorus and Heavy Metals (AOAC 2015.01)
- **Digestion:** Microwave-assisted acid digestion ($6\text{ mL } \text{HNO}_3 + 1\text{ mL } \text{H}_2\text{O}_2$) in sealed PTFE vessels at $200^\circ\text{C}$ for 20 minutes.
- **Instrument:** Agilent 7900 ICP-MS with octopole collision cell (helium mode).
- **Target Analytes:** Phosphorus ($m/z = 31$), Lead ($m/z = 208$), Cadmium ($m/z = 111$), Arsenic ($m/z = 75$).
- **Quantification Limit (LOQ):** $P = 1.0\text{ mg/kg}$; heavy metals $< 0.01\text{ mg/kg}$.

### 4.2 HPLC-UV Protocol for Organic Acids (AOAC 986.13)
- **Extraction:** Aqueous homogenization of ground dal in $0.05\text{ M } \text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$, centrifugal clarification ($10,000 \times g$ for 15 min), and $0.22\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ PTFE filtration.
- **Column:** Aminex HPX-87H ion-exclusion column ($300 \times 7.8\text{ mm}$ ID).
- **Mobile Phase:** $0.005\text{ M } \text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$ isocratic at $0.6\text{ mL/min}$, column temperature $50^\circ\text{C}$.
- **Detection:** UV absorbance at $\lambda = 210\text{ nm}$.
- **Retention Time:** Citric acid elutes sharply at $7.82\text{ minutes}$.

### 4.3 ISO 4120 Sensory Triangle Testing (3-AFC Protocol)
- **Panel Design:** 30 trained panellists tested under blind conditions (red lighting to mask visual cues, standardized 3-digit blinding codes).
- **Presentation:** Two control samples (cooked untreated domestic desi dal) vs. one test sample (cooked FinnoaQ treated dal), or vice-versa, randomized order.
- **Statistical Significance Threshold:** $\alpha = 0.05$. In a 30-panellist test, the null hypothesis (no detectable sensory difference) is rejected only if $\ge 15$ panellists identify the odd sample.
- **Measured Result:** FinnoaQ batches consistently register **11 to 13 correct selections** ($p = 0.38–0.45$), confirming **no statistically significant sensory difference** from the domestic reference.
