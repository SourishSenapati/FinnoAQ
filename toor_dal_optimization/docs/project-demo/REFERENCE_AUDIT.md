# Engineering Design & Digital Editorial Reference Audit

**Project Reference:** FNQ-PR-2026-001  
**Project:** Imported Split Pigeon Pea Cooking-Time Reduction & 15 MT/day Conceptual Plant Engineering  
**Audit Date:** 2026-10-17  

---

## 1. Industrial Benchmark References

The design of this single-project engineering microsite draws structural inspiration from tier-1 industrial processing technology leaders:

### 1.1 Bühler Group (Grain & Food Processing)

- **Observed Structure:** Direct, factual narrative leading with grain pathology and moisture dynamics; clear unit operation sequences without decorative clutter; technical specifications presented in tabular formats.
- **Applied to Project:** Candidate process flow shown as explicit unit operations (Intake -> Characterization -> Screening -> Dewatering -> Fluid Bed Drying -> Cooking Test -> Plant Scale-up); equipment cards pairing genuine CAD isometric projections with key mechanical parameters.

### 1.2 GEA Group (Process Engineering & Evaporation)

- **Observed Structure:** Distinction between pilot/screening validation and commercial scale plant engineering; focus on mass balance closure and thermal utility demand.
- **Applied to Project:** Clear separation between Phase 1 diagnostic laboratory screening and Phase 2 15 MT/day conceptual plant engineering.

### 1.3 Alfa Laval (Thermal & Separation Systems)

- **Observed Structure:** Equipment general arrangements paired with functional service descriptions and operating limits.
- **Applied to Project:** Component breakdowns for TK-101, SC-101, DR-101, CY-101, and EV-101 with explicit materials of construction (SS316L wetted parts) and operating envelopes.

---

## 2. Digital Editorial References (Hierarchy, Pacing, Contrast)

- **Stripe & Linear:** Applied strictly for clear typographic hierarchy, disciplined alignment grids, responsive navigation with visible active states, and restrained contrast. No arbitrary neon gradients, no bubbly cards, no floating blur filters.
- **Vercel Documentation:** Applied for structured technical callouts, explicit status labeling, and fast local asset rendering.

---

## 3. Explicit Anti-Patterns Enforced

- NO emojis anywhere in UI or documentation.
- NO generic marketing fluff ("trusted by Fortune 500", fake client logos, artificial testimonial carousels).
- NO fake live SCADA telemetry or animated sensor meters claiming real-time plant connection.
- NO synthetic CAD imitations (e.g., Python PIL/Matplotlib coordinate line sketches pretending to be engineering drawings).
- NO claims that treatment formulations or factory construction are physically validated.
