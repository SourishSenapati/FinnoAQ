Client Inputs Required to Initiate 48-Hour Trial

To commence Phase 1 diagnostic testing on your imported pigeon pea lot, provide the following parameters:

  • 1
    Representative Grain Sample: Delivery of 5 to 10 kg of representative imported split pigeon pea (Toor Dal) with lot provenance, harvest season, and port intake details.
  • 2
    Current Boiling / Whistle Latency: Baseline boiling duration or domestic pressure cooker whistle count required for acceptable culinary tenderness.
  • 3
    Target Daily Tolling Volume: Confirmation of desired mill processing volume (1 TPD pilot, 5 TPD modular, or 15 TPD turnkey commercial plant).
