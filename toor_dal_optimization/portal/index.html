Client Inputs Required to Initiate Study

To commence Phase 1 diagnostic testing, the following inputs and authorizations are required from the client organization:

  • 1
    Representative Target Grain Sample: Delivery of 10 to 15 kg of representative imported split pigeon pea (Toor Dal) with lot provenance, harvest season, and port intake details.
  • 2
    Baseline Milling & Quality Standards: Existing mill processing flow, typical baseline boiling time (minutes), acceptable split integrity thresholds, and target moisture specifications.
  • 3
    Operational Shift Pattern: Confirmation of desired factory operating profile (Single shift 8h, Two shifts 16h, or Continuous 24h) for conceptual sizing alignment.
  • 4
    Authorized Technical Liaison: Designation of a single point of contact for weekly progress updates and final presentation review.
