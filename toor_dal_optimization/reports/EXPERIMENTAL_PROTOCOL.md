# FINNOAQ TOOR DAL — STANDARDIZED EXPERIMENTAL PROTOCOL

**Document ID:** HKT-PRT-2026-001  
**Preregistered Confirmatory Assay:** 4-Arm Randomized Block Cooking Evaluation  
**Standard:** Stage 1 Empirical Gate Verification  
**Apparatus:** Hawkins Classic 3.0 L Pressure Cooker (115 kPa Weighted Regulator) + 1200W Induction Cooktop  
**Date:** 2026-10-14  

---

## 1. Experimental Objectives & Arm Allocations

This protocol provides the standardized physical procedure to evaluate whether the proposed treatment successfully reduces cooking time to $\le 15.0\text{ minutes}$ ($\le 3.5\text{ whistles}$) while preserving grain integrity.

### Treatment Arms ($n = 4$ Independent Batches Each)
1. **Arm 1 (Control):** Untreated Tanzanian Dal, soaked in potable water (1.5:1 ratio, 30 min, 25°C), drained, dried at 45°C.
2. **Arm 2 (Low-Dose):** 0.25% w/w Food-Grade NaHCO₃ (1.5:1 ratio, 30 min, 25°C), drained, 10s mist rinse, dried at 45°C.
3. **Arm 3 (Nominal Winner):** **0.50% w/w Food-Grade NaHCO₃** (1.5:1 ratio, 30 min, 25°C), drained, 10s mist rinse, dried at 45°C.
4. **Arm 4 (Citrate Alternative):** 0.50% w/w Food-Grade Sodium Citrate (1.5:1 ratio, 30 min, 25°C), drained, 10s mist rinse, dried at 45°C.

---

## 2. Standardized Apparatus & Cooking Conditions

To ensure reproducible thermal boundary conditions:
- **Cooker:** Hawkins Classic 3.0 Litre aluminum/SS pressure cooker with calibrated 1.0 kg/cm² (15 psi / 103–115 kPa) weighted vent weight.
- **Heating Source:** Commercial induction cooktop set to **1200 Watts** constant power (eliminates domestic LPG flame variability).
- **Dal Sample Charge:** $100.0 \pm 0.1\text{ grams}$ dry dal per run.
- **Cooking Water Charge:** $300.0 \pm 1.0\text{ grams}$ potable water ($3.0 : 1.0\text{ liquid-to-solid ratio}$) at ambient $25.0 \pm 1.0^\circ\text{C}$.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Test Procedure

```
[ STEP 1: CHARGE ] ──► Place 100.0g dal + 300.0g water into cooker. Lock lid.
                               │
[ STEP 2: HEAT ]    ──► Place cooker on 1200W induction; start digital timer (t = 0).
                               │
[ STEP 3: INDUCTION]──► Record timestamp t_steam when continuous steam hisses from vent pipe
                        (nominal 3.8 to 4.5 min). Place vent weight on vent pipe.
                               │
[ STEP 4: WHISTLES] ──► Record exact stopwatch timestamp for Whistle 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.
                               │
[ STEP 5: CUTOFF ]  ──► For Arms 2, 3, 4: Cut heat at Whistle 3.5 (or elapsed t = 15.0 min).
                        For Arm 1 (Control): Cut heat at Whistle 6.0 (or elapsed t = 58.0 min).
                               │
[ STEP 6: COOLING ] ──► Allow exactly 5.0 minutes of natural cooling; lift weight to depressurize.
                               │
[ STEP 7: SQUASH ]  ──► Extract 10 cotyledons from core. Compress between two glass slides under
                        1.0 kg dead weight. If 10/10 cotyledons spread into smooth paste with
                        zero hard white center, mark as COOKED.
```
