## 2. Four-Stage Technology Architecture & Scope Boundaries

To prevent misunderstanding or unapproved liabilities, the four successive phases of the Toor Dal engineering program are formally decoupled:

| Phase | Designation | Core Technical Objective | Commercial Status | Inclusions | Exclusions (Strict Scope Gates) |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1** | **Comparative Diagnostic R&D Study** | Characterize target lot vs. Indian benchmark; measure physical, proximate, thermal, hydration, and cooking differences; discriminate across 10 hypotheses. | **Active Contract Scope (₹1,73,000 + GST)** | Diagnostic laboratory tests, comparative benchmark matrix, root-cause assessment, Phase 2 roadmap. | **Excludes:** Formulation optimization, chemical treatment screening trials, pilot toll manufacturing, shelf-life verification, commercial plant engineering. |
| **Phase 2** | Treatment Validation & Formulation Optimization | Screen chemical/thermal conditioning agents (bicarbonate, citrate, hydrothermal); optimize dose, pH, drying, and storage stability. | Gated Future Scope (Budget conditional on Phase 1 findings) | Bench-scale soaking trials, kinetic modeling, sensory evaluation, sodium retention assay, washing losses. | Commercial factory construction, industrial equipment procurement. |
| **Phase 3** | Pilot Production & Shelf-Life Verification | Continuous pilot runs in representative equipment; test microbiological stability during steeping/drying; packaging qualification; effluent load. | Future Milestone (Gated on Phase 2 validation) | Pilot toll runs, consumer sensory panel, 12-month ambient storage stability, real wastewater characterization. | Full-scale commercial site construction. |
| **Phase 4** | 15,000 kg/day Plant Design & Execution | Complete Front-End Engineering Design (FEED), procurement specifications, civil/structural layout, installation, commissioning. | Future Commercial Investment | Construction-ready drawings, equipment vendor selection, turnkey EPC execution. | All guarantees are locked until Phases 1–3 data validate the physical process route. |

### 3.1 Commercial Terms, Taxes, and Ceilings
- **Total Fixed Professional Fee:** **₹1,73,000 (Rupees One Lakh Seventy-Three Thousand Only)**.
- **Goods & Services Tax (GST):** GST is applicable extra at prevailing statutory rates (currently $18\%$ under SAC 998313 — Scientific and Technical R&D Services). Billed on actuals.
- **Commercial Ceiling:** **Up to ₹2,00,000 + applicable GST** (source SOW Table 42). Any additional exploratory testing beyond the 11 scoped items requires written client change orders before exceeding ₹1,73,000.
- **Fee Inclusions:** Covers all protocol design, laboratory coordination, internal physical/cooking/texture testing, data synthesis, statistical analysis, and engineering reporting. External accredited laboratory testing charges for specialized modules (DSC, ICP-OES, cell-wall fractions) are coordinated within this budget framework based on candidate provider quotations.
