## 3. Unlock Criteria for Commercial Execution

Stage 2 will automatically transition from `LOCKED` to `ACTIVE` when:
1. Four independent replicate physical batches of Candidate A2 (0.50% NaHCO₃) achieve a mean cooking time of $\le 15.0\text{ minutes}$ ($\le 3.5\text{ whistles}$) in the Hawkins 3.0L cooker.
2. The glass plate squash test confirms $10/10$ cotyledons spread into a smooth paste without a hard core.
3. The sensory review confirms zero soapy, alkaline, or chemical off-notes.
4. Laboratory certification timestamps are committed to `data/raw/`.
