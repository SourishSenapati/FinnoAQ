## 1. Candidate Screening Funnel Overview

Every candidate generated by the design space engine was passed through 5 successive screening layers:
* **Layer 1: Hard Constraints:** Rejects non-FSSAI additives, prohibited chemicals, equipment safety limits, and pyrolysis temperatures ($T > 220^\circ\text{C}$).
* **Layer 2: Scientific & Transport Bounds:** Rejects excessive moisture additions ($>30\%$) causing cotyledon slurry collapse.
* **Layer 3: Soft Predictive Quality:** Rejects candidates resulting in severe available lysine loss ($<82\%$) or unacceptable texture ($>28\text{ N}$).
* **Layer 4: Techno-Economics:** Rejects candidates with added OPEX $> \text{INR } 2.50\text{/kg}$.
* **Layer 5: Robustness Across Feedstock Lots:** Tests stability across variable initial moisture and grain size.
