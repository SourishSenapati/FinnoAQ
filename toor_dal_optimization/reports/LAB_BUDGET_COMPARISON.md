## 2. CAPITAL PRESERVATION & HARDWARE REALLOCATION ANALYSIS

Instead of spending funds on redundant laboratory test tubes whose outcomes are already bounded by literature, this capital can be permanently converted into high-performance R&D computing infrastructure:

- **Desktop Workstation Upgrade:**
  - Dedicated NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4080 (16 GB GDDR6X) or RTX 4070 Ti Super (16 GB GDDR6X) + 64 GB DDR5 RAM + Ryzen 9 7900X CPU (~₹1.85 – ₹2.05 lakh).
- **Engineering ROI:**
  - A permanent workstation compounds capabilities across all future chemical engineering, CFD, heat/mass transfer, and Bayesian optimization projects over the next 5+ years.
  - An outsourced lab report depreciates to zero once read.
