### 3.1 Commercial Terms, Taxes, and Ceilings
- **Total Proposed Professional Fee:** **₹1,73,000 (Rupees One Lakh Seventy-Three Thousand Only)**.
- **Applicable Taxes:** GST is applicable extra at prevailing statutory rates (currently $18\%$ under SAC 998313 — Scientific and Technical R&D Services). Exact tax is billed on actuals; no rate is arbitrarily assumed.
- **Suggested Commercial Ceiling:** **Up to ₹2,00,000 + applicable GST** (source SOW Table 42). Any additional exploratory tests beyond the scoped 11 items require prior written client change orders before exceeding ₹1,73,000.
- **Cost Inclusions:** The fee covers all protocol design, laboratory coordination, internal physical/cooking/texture testing, data synthesis, statistical analysis, and engineering reporting. External accredited laboratory testing charges for specialized modules (DSC, ICP-OES, cell-wall fractions) are coordinated within this budget framework based on candidate provider quotations.
