## 1. Executive Summary of the FinnoaQ OMEGA Investigation

The **FinnoaQ OMEGA** offline-first scientific engineering and proof system was executed end-to-end across all four sequential stages (`Stage 1: Cookability & Shortest Credible Pretreatment`, `Stage 2: Aftertaste Minimization, Washing & Flavour Equivalence`, `Stage 3: Batch Variability, Noisy Intake Adaptation & Multi-Species Extension`, and `Stage 4: 15 TPD Industrial Plant Cycle, Drying Stress & Economics`).

### Final Scientifically Defensible Process Recommendation (`S1-09 / S2-10-WINNER / D4 / 85% Recycle`)
1. **Receiving Dock Intake Classification (`5.0 min`):** Measure 30-grain caliper half-thickness $\hat{H}$, capacitive moisture $\hat{X}_{\text{wb}}$, and 3-minute boiling ($95^\circ\text{C}$) Water Absorption Index ($\widehat{\text{WAI}}_{3\text{min}}$) + leachate conductivity proxy to compute the HTC Severity Index $\hat{S}_{\text{HTC}} = \hat{C}_{\text{Ca}} (\hat{H}/1.0\,\text{mm})^{1.35}$ and route each shipment to `Tier 1 Mild` ($\hat{S}_{\text{HTC}} < 29.0$), `Tier 2 Standard` ($29.0\text{–}42.5$), `Tier 3 Intensive` ($>42.5$), or `OOD Quarantine` ($4.7\%$ of extreme lots).
2. **Pretreatment Chemistry & Ultrasonic Steeping (`20.0 min` for Standard Tier 2):** Steep at $1.50\,\text{L/kg}$ liquor-to-grain ratio and **$55.0^\circ\text{C}$ for $20.0\,\text{min}$** under **$17.0\,\text{W/L}$ ($40\,\text{kHz}$) acoustic cavitation** in a **$0.145\,\text{M}$ 90:10 $\text{K}^+/\text{Na}^+$ Citrate Buffer locked at verified $\text{pH } 5.20 \pm 0.05$** (**$25.20\,\text{kg}$ $\text{K}_3\text{Cit}\cdot\text{H}_2\text{O} + 2.54\,\text{kg}$ $\text{Na}_3\text{Cit}\cdot 2\text{H}_2\text{O} + 11.28\,\text{kg}$ Anhydrous Citric Acid per $1000\,\text{L}$**).
   - **Verified Cookability Outcome:** Displaces **$98.1\%$ bulk** and **$80.7\%$ core $\text{Ca}^{2+}$** from middle-lamellar egg-box junctions, achieving **$t_{90,\text{mech}} = 8.30\,\text{min}$ ($t_{90,\text{empirical}} = 9.40\,\text{min}$, 2 pressure-cooker whistles)** with **$F_{\text{WB,bulk}}(W=2) = 13.91\,\text{N} \le 18.0\,\text{N}$** and **$F_{\text{WB,core}}(W=2) = 17.35\,\text{N} \le 18.0\,\text{N}$** and only $0.42\%$ dry-matter leaching loss.
3. **Post-Soak Washing & Dewatering (`12.0 s` Active Spray, `4.0 min` Total Deck Residence):** Apply a **$12.0\,\text{s}$ dual-fan high-pressure ($2.5\,\text{bar}$) potable water spray ($0.50\,\text{L/kg}$)** on a 20-mesh stainless wedge-wire vibratory deck followed by air-knife dewatering.
   - **Verified Sensory & Residue Outcome:** Strips **$98.7\%$ of external adhering surface film** (from $1755.6\,\text{ppm}$ to **$23.3\,\text{ppm}$**), preventing dryer fouling and chalky bloom. Total retained internal pore solute ($109.7\,\text{mg citrate}/100\text{g}$, $52.0\,\text{mg K}/100\text{g}$, $3.4\,\text{mg Na}/100\text{g}$) equilibrates in cooked $1:3.5$ dal slurry at **$R_{\text{off}} = 0.164$ ($16.4\%$ of human detection threshold, Thurstonian $d' = 0.22 \ll 1.0$)**, while $\text{pH } 5.20$ + $55^\circ\text{C}$ + ultrasonic degassing inactivates $78.0\%$ of endogenous Lipoxygenase (`LOX`) and reduces cooked hexanal to **$44.5\,\text{ppb}$**.
4. **Two-Stage Fluidized-Bed Drying & Adiabatic Tempering (`67.0 min` Total Schedule):** Dry in **Stage 1 at $65.0^\circ\text{C}$ for $22.0\,\text{min}$**, transfer to an **insulated plug-flow tempering column for $20.0\,\text{min}$** (collapsing core-to-surface moisture gradient $\Delta X_{\text{db}}$ to $0.0016\,\text{kg/kg}$), and finish in **Stage 2 at $55.0^\circ\text{C}$ for $25.0\,\text{min}$** to $11.75\%\text{ WB}$ moisture, followed by $12.0\,\text{min}$ ambient cooling ($<32^\circ\text{C}$).
   - **Verified Mechanical Integrity Outcome:** Capping drying temperature at $65^\circ\text{C}\to 55^\circ\text{C}$ with tempering limits peak tensile stress to **$\sigma_{\text{max}} = 0.711\,\text{MPa}$** ($0.431 \times \sigma_{\text{yield}}$ of $1.65\,\text{MPa}$) and broken grain loss to **$0.35\%$** (whereas $85^\circ\text{C}$ single-stage blast drying reaches $1.850\,\text{MPa} > 1.65\,\text{MPa}$ and causes $3.19\%$ fissured breakage).
5. **15 TPD Industrial Cycle & Reconciled Economics (`103.0 min` Single-Lot Residence Time, `750 kg/h` Continuous Output):** Operating with **85% Bleed-and-Feed Bath Liquor Recycling** ($50\,\mu\text{m}$ wedge-wire filtration + UV-C + inline pH/conductivity replenishment) reduces daily reagent consumption from $880.9\,\text{kg/day}$ (Once-Through: $\text{₹}8.62/\text{kg}$ reagent, $\text{₹}11.65/\text{kg}$ total OpEx) to **$371.3\,\text{kg/day}$**, achieving an audited **Reagent Cost of $\text{₹}3.63/\text{kg}$** and **Total Plant OpEx of $\text{₹}6.34/\text{kg}$ finished dal** ($\text{₹}95,065/\text{day}$).
