## 1. Executive Cost Summary

This economic assessment details the net incremental operating expenditure (OpEx) and preliminary capital expenditure (CapEx) for adding a 15 MT/day chemical conditioning line to Harakutir's pulse milling facility.

### Key Economic Indicators (Base Case: 15,000 kg/day Feed, 16h Schedule)
- **Net Incremental Processing OpEx:** **₹1.336 per kg** of finished saleable packaged dal.
- **Daily Operating Expenditure:** **₹19,179 per day** (based on $14,353.5\text{ kg/day}$ packaged output).
- **Annual Operating Expenditure (300 operating days):** **₹57.54 Lakhs / year** ($0.575\text{ Crore INR}$).
- **Estimated Turnkey Capital Cost (CapEx):** **₹68.50 Lakhs** ($0.685\text{ Crore INR}$).
- **Simple Payback Period:** **$5.7\text{ months}$** at a conservative consumer retail premium of ₹3.00/kg.
- **Break-Even Consumer Premium Required:** **₹1.34 / kg** (above raw untreated dal price).

## 4. Sensitivity Tornado Analysis

The relative impact of individual cost drivers on the final processing cost per kg of dal is shown below:

```
                                  [ SENSITIVITY TORNADO: IMPACT ON OPEX ]
                                           (Base Cost: ₹1.34 / kg)
                                                      │
  Thermal Fuel Tariff (±35%)        [ -₹0.12 ]        ├───[ +₹0.22 ]
                                                      │
  Solids Recovery (94.2% to 96.2%)  [ -₹0.08 ]        ├───[ +₹0.14 ]
                                                      │
  Reagent Bulk Price (±25%)         [ -₹0.04 ]        ├───[ +₹0.07 ]
                                                      │
  Electricity Tariff (±15%)         [ -₹0.02 ]        ├───[ +₹0.02 ]
                                                      │
  Wastewater Treatment (±30%)       [ -₹0.02 ]        ├───[ +₹0.02 ]
                                                      │
                                   ◄──────────────────┴──────────────────►
                                 -₹0.15   -₹0.10   0.00   +0.10   +0.20
                                              DELTA OPEX (₹/kg Dal)
```

**Key Takeaways:**
1. **Thermal Drying Fuel is the #1 Cost Driver:** Accounting for $43.8\%$ of total OpEx, fluctuations in biomass briquette price or boiler efficiency have twice the impact of chemical price changes.
2. **Solids Recovery is the #2 Economic Driver:** A $1.5\%$ drop in whole-plant recovery due to fines or excessive leaching increases net cost by ₹0.14/kg due to lost saleable product volume.
3. **Chemical Cost is Secondary:** Because the required dose is modest ($0.50\%$), chemical cost represents only $20.6\%$ of OpEx.
