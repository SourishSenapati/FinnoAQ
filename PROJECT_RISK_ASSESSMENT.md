## 1. Mustard Oil (The "Herbal Fusion")

### Mustard Oil Critical Problems

1. **Sensory Overload (The "Red" Problem):**
   - _Issue:_ The simulation optimized **Spent Chilli Oil to 11.1%** to save money.
   - _Risk:_ At equal pungency to mustard, Chilli Oil is **RED**. 11% will turn the oil distinctively orange-red, not golden-yellow. Consumers will think it's adulterated with palm/chemicals.
   - _Mitigation:_ **HARD CAP Chilli Oil at 3-5%**. Use **Spent Turmeric (0.5%)** to correct the red hue back to golden yellow.

2. **Regulatory Compliance (AITC Level):**
   - _Issue:_ Simulation result: **0.54% AITC**.
   - _Target:_ > 0.55% (minimum for strong "Jhal" perception).
   - _Risk:_ FSSAI testing variance could flag this as substandard.
   - _Mitigation:_ Increase **Natural AITC Booster** dosage by 10% (from 800ppm to 880ppm). Cost impact is negligible (<₹0.05/kg) but ensures safety margin.

3. **Viscosity (The "Watery" Feel):**
   - _Issue:_ Result **45.5 cP** (Target 50 cP).
   - _Risk:_ Oil pours too "fast". Consumers associate thickness with purity.
   - _Mitigation:_ Maintain **Cottonseed Oil > 55%**. Cottonseed is naturally thick. Do NOT switch to Palm Olein (35 cP) even if cheaper, as it ruins the "body".

4. **Labeling Trap:**
   - _Issue:_ Blend contains only 33% Mustard.
   - _Risk:_ Inspecting officers seizing stock for "Misbranding" if "Mustard" font is too big.
   - _Mitigation:_ Brand name must be abstract (e.g., "Kachi Ghani _Taste_"). The FSSAI Category "Multi-Source Edible Oil" must be the largest descriptor.

## 2. Multigrain Atta (The "Jugaad" Staple)

### Multigrain Atta Critical Problems

1. **The "Jeera Roti" Disaster (Sensory):**
   - _Issue:_ Simulation used **5.3% Spent Cumin** to hit fiber targets cheaply.
   - _Risk:_ Cumin has a dominant, earthy flavor. At 5%, everyday rotis will taste like "Masala Roti". Users will reject it for eating with Dal/Sabzi.
   - _Mitigation:_ **HARD CAP Spent Cumin at 1.0%**. It is a flavor contaminant at high levels.

2. **Gluten Collapse (Texture):**
   - _Issue:_ **40% Non-Wheat Flours** (Maize 20% + Spices 14% + Millets).
   - _Risk:_ Gluten dilution. Dough will break; Rotis will be "papad-like" (brittle) and dry out in 1 hour.
   - _Mitigation:_ Add **0.5% Guar Gum** or **2% Vital Wheat Gluten** (Cost: +₹1.5/kg). This is non-negotiable for 40% dilution.

3. **Fenugreek Bitterness Limit:**
   - _Issue:_ **7.6% Spent Fenugreek**.
   - _Risk:_ Even de-bitterized fenugreek has an aftertaste. 7.6% is the theoretical maximum.
   - _Mitigation:_ **Pilot Test:** Make 1kg batch. If bitter, reduce to 5% and accept lower fiber (4%), or sell as "Diabetic Atta" (Bitter is expected).

4. **Profit Trap (The ₹30 Ceiling):**
   - _Issue:_ Factory Cost ₹25.15. Retail ₹30. **Margin ₹4.85**.
   - _Risk:_ Standard Retailer margin is ₹5-8. Distributor needs ₹2. You are mathematically **LOSS-MAKING** in general trade.
   - _Mitigation:_
     - **Strategy A (D2C Only):** Sell online/local delivery only. Keep the ₹30 USP.
     - **Strategy B (Price Correction):** Rebrand as "Super-Fiber Diabetic Atta" and price at **₹45/kg**. This grants a massive ₹20 margin.
