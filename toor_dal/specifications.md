# Toor Dal Analogue: Technical Specifications and Production Feasibility Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary

This document outlines the technical specifications, formulation strategy, and cost analysis for the industrial production of a Toor Dal (Pigeon Pea) Analogue. The primary objective is to manufacture a cost-effective, high-fidelity analogue that replicates the rheological, organoleptic, and cooking characteristics of natural Toor Dal. The formulation incorporates a specific starch-protein-biopolymer matrix optimized for extrusion processing, ensuring structural integrity and consumer acceptability while achieving a target production cost of ₹68.69/kg.

## 2. Optimized Formulation and Cost Structure

## 4. Technical Specifications and Process Parameters

### 4.1 Dry Feed Rheology

- **Particle Size Distribution**: Ball-milled to <150 µm to ensure uniform hydration and a smooth protein-starch matrix.
- **Matrix Modifiers**:
  - **Glycerol Monostearate (E471)**: 1.0% inclusion to induce amylose-lipid complexation, reducing extrudate stickiness and improving bite texture.
  - **Sodium Alginate + Calcium Lactate**: Forms a heat-stable ionic gel preventing disintegration during boiling.

### 4.2 Hydration and Extrusion

- **Target Moisture Content**: 30% ± 2% (wet basis).
- **Water Addition Rate**: 300 ml per kg of dry mix.
- **pH Control**: Adjusted to 5.5–6.0 using Citric Acid to optimize alginate setting kinetics and Nisin stability.
- **Thermal Profile**:
  - **Pre-conditioning**: 80–90°C (Partial gelatinization).
  - **Extrusion Die Temperature**: ~60°C (Prevents premature alginate cross-linking).
  - **Die Geometry**: 3.0–4.0 mm circular aperture.

### 4.3 Preservation System

- **Internal (Phase A)**: Nisin A (12.5 ppm pure basis) targeting _Bacillus cereus_ spore inhibition.
- **Surface Coating (Phase B)**: Post-drying application (<40°C) of a hydrophobic blend comprising 98% Refined Castor Oil and 2% Clove Oil (0.5–1.0% w/w application rate). This provides a matte "unpolished" finish and improved oxidative stability.

### 4.4 Drying Dynamics

- **Method**: Vacuum Drying @ 60°C.
- **Target Final Moisture**: <10%.
- **Constraint**: Controlled drying rate required to prevent stress cracking and checking in the final product.

## 5. Regulatory Compliance

- **MSG Limits**: The 0.35% inclusion rate adheres to FSSAI Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) for processed cereal-pulse analogues.
- **Labeling**: Ingredient declaration required ("Contains Added Monosodium Glutamate").
- **Validation**: Sensory and microbial stability validation to be conducted on finished product after a minimum 7-day maturation period.

## 6. Phase 1 - Mathematical Screening Results

A stochastic simulation (100,000 Monte Carlo iterations) was conducted to identify the optimal formulation window balancing cost and nutritional integrity.

**Narrowed Formulation Band (Successful in 22.6% of theoretical combinations):**

- **Tur Khanda**: 50.0% – 55.0% (Mean: 53.3%)
- **Starch Filler**: 40.0% – 45.0% (Mean: 42.3%)
- **Alginate**: 0.60% – 0.85% (Mean: 0.72%) [Significantly reduced from 1.2%]
- **Protein Isolate**: 0.80% – 1.20% (Mean: 0.94%)
- **Surface Oil**: ~0.45%
- **Process Loss**: ~1.0%

**Financial & Technical Outcomes:**

- **Projected Ex-Factory Cost**: ₹ 65.30 / kg (vs. ₹68.69 Baseline)
- **Projected Raw Material Cost**: ₹ 56.02 / kg (vs. ₹59.41 Baseline)
- **Protein Content**: ~15.5% (Acceptable Analogue Standard)
- **Sensitivity**: Raw Material Price (Tur + Alginate) remains the dominant cost driver.

## 7. Phase 2 - Bench Validation Simulation (Manual Variability Stress Test)

To simulate manual variability in low-cost R&D conditions (Mixie grinding, manual mixing, tray drying), a Monte Carlo simulation (100,000 batches) was conducted using the Phase 1 optimized parameters.

**Results:**

- **Success Rate**: 78.36% (Below 80% Target)
- **Failure Mode**: 18.06% "Soup Risk" (Disintegration during boiling due to uneven binder distribution).
- **Root Cause**: Manual mixing coefficient of variation (CV ~20%) creates weak points in the dough matrix where local Alginate < 0.5%.

**Corrective Action for Physical Trials:**

- **Validation Formulation Adjustment**: Increase Alginate from **0.72%** to **0.85%** specifically for manual bench trials.
- **Why**: This provides a "Safety Factor" against manual mixing errors without invalidating the cost model for industrial production (where mixing is perfect).
- **Revised Bench Trial Cost**: ₹260 / batch (Well within ₹1.2 Lakh budget).

## 8. Phase 3 - Contract Extrusion Validation (Virtual Twin)

A high-volume (1,000,000 run) GPU simulation calibrated the operating window for a standard Twin Screw Extruder to validate feasibility without buying the machine (saving ₹3.5L CapEx).

**Feasibility Confirmed:**

- **Success Rate**: 41.5% of random machine settings worked (Wide Operating Window).
- **CaPEx Decision**: **DO NOT BUY EXTRUDER.** The process is robust enough to run on a rental machine with high confidence.

**Recommended Rental Machine Settings:**

- **Screw Speed**: ~350 RPM
- **Feed Rate**: ~32 kg/hr
- **Barrel Temperature**: 120–125°C (Critical: Keep Die Cooled < 80°C to prevent puffing)
- **Expected SME**: ~113 kJ/kg (Perfect for "Cooked but Dense" texture)
- **Die Pressure**: ~74 Bar (Stable flow)

**Risk**: Expansion Ratio is low (~1.09). Product might be too dense.

- **Mitigation**: Ensure "Hot Plate Hydration" in Phase 2 is thorough to pre-soften starch.
