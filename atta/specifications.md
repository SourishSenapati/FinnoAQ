# Multigrain Atta (Natural & Spice-Enriched) Project Specifications

## 1. Executive Summary

Produce premium **Multigrain Atta** at a target retail price of **₹30/kg** (vs Market ₹40-60) using a "Jugaad" formulation.
**Update (Feb 2026):** Sensory correction applied. Usage of spent spice fibers limited to 11% total (was 36%) to ensure **zero bitterness** and **natural color**. Cost impact absorbed by optimizing Maize/Bajra ratios.

## 2. "Jugaad" Formulation Strategy (Sensory Validated 6-Sigma)

- **Base Blend (55%)**:
  - Soft Wheat (Lokwan): ~45% (Market Price ₹25/kg)
  - Hard Wheat (Sharbati): ~5% (Strength)
  - Gluten/Starch Network preserved.

- **Multigrain Mix (34%)**:
  - Maize (Makka): **20.0%** (Max Limit Hit - Primary Cost Reducer)
  - Bajra/Jowar: ~14.0% (Nutritional Fillers)
  - Soybean (Non-GMO): 0.1% (Trace)
- **The Innovation (11% - Sensory Safe Limits)**:
  - **Spent Fenugreek Fiber**: **8.0%** (Capped. Must be _Debitterized_).
  - **Spent Turmeric Fiber**: **3.0%** (Capped. "Golden Glow" without neon yellow).
  - _Result_: Raw material cost adjusted to **₹20.50/kg**.

## 4. Machinery Requirements

### A. Lab Scale (R&D / Pilot)

- **Capacity**: 5-10 kg/hr
- **Equipment**:
  1. **Stone Burr Mill (Chakki)**: 1 HP (For traditional texture).
  2. **Planetary Mixer**: 10L (For uniform blending of spice fibers).
  3. **Vibratory Sifter**: manual feed (To separate coarse bran).
- **Capex**: ~₹85,000

### B. Industrial Scale (Production)

- **Capacity**: 2000 kg/hr (2 Ton/hr)
- **Equipment**:
  1. **Cleaning Section**: Destoner, Scourer, Magnetic Separator.
  2. **Milling**: 4-Pass Roller Mill (Break) + 2-Pass Reduction + Plate Mill (Chakki finish).
  3. **Conditioning**: High-dampening Tempering Bins (12hr hold).
  4. **Blending**: Ribbon Blender (2000L) for Spice Fiber integration.
  5. **Packing**: FFS Pneumatic Packer.
- **Capex**: ~₹1.5 Crores
