# FinnoAQ Industrial Master Operational Overview (V3.0)

## 1. High-Level Facility Architecture
FinnoAQ is a Multi-Stream Industrial Food Processing facility. It is divided into three distinct operational blocks:
- **Block A (The Hot Zone):** Honey Wine (Mead), Honey Toffy, Honey Jelly. (Energy focus: Steam/Heating).
- **Block B (The Milling Zone):** Chakki Atta, Toor Dal, Mustard Oil. (Energy focus: Heavy Motors/Vibration).
- **Block C (The Cold Zone):** Bilona Ghee (Danedar Setting), Honey Creme (Dyce Cycle), Sundarban Honey. (Energy focus: Refrigeration/Vacuum).

## 2. Integrated Utility Load-Balancing (24-Hr Cycle)
To minimize peak-load tariffs and prevent boiler overload, the lines are synchronized as follows:

| Shift | Primary Operations (Heavy Load) | Secondary Operations (Low Load) | Energy Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shift 1 (06:00-14:00)** | Atta & Dal Milling; Oil Expelling. | Honey Jelly/Toffy Prep. | Peak motor load during daytime. |
| **Shift 2 (14:00-22:00)** | Toffy Boiling; Spray Drying (Powder). | Filling & Packaging. | Max steam usage. Shared boiler header. |
| **Shift 3 (22:00-06:00)** | Cold Room Ops (Creme/Ghee/Mead). | In-process Lab QC/Titration. | Use night-time off-peak electricity tariffs for Chillers. |

## 4. Supply Chain & Logistics (West Bengal Inbound/Outbound)
- **Inbound (Raw):** 
  - **Milk:** Daily 10kL (A2 Dairy clusters - Nadia/Hooghly).
  - **Wheat/Dal:** Bulk 50 MT/Week (Burdwan/Bankura Mandis).
  - **Raw Honey:** Collection centers in Haroa & Sunderbans (Monthly 50-100 MT).
- **Outbound (Finished):**
  - **Main Hub:** Kolkata (Centralized Distribution).
  - **Export Route:** Haldia Port (Containerized shipping for Mead/Sundarban Honey).

## 5. Master First-Batch Validation Template (MPR-001)
*To be filled by the Plant Head for the first production run of each product.*

- **[ ] Physical Audit:** All SS-316 surfaces pass 'Allergen-Free' swab test (Mustard trace < 5ppm).
- **[ ] Energy Calibration:** Total peak load check (must not cross sanctioned 450kVA).

- **[ ] Lab Verification:** COA (Certificate of Analysis) attached for pH, Moisture, and Micro-count.
