# Honey Jelly Industrial Production: Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

## 1. Process Overview and Gelation Chemistry
Honey jelly is produced via High-Methoxyl (HM) Pectin gelation at high soluble solids (Brix 65%+) and acidic conditions (pH 3.1-3.3). This creates a thermo-stable 3D carbohydrate network that encapsulates honey and water.

### 1.1 Target Specifications
- **Total Soluble Solids (TSS):** 65 - 68 Brix
- **PH Range:** 3.1 - 3.3
- **Bloom Strength (Gel Strength):** 110 g - 150 g
- **Shelf Life:** 12 - 18 Months (Stable @ 30°C)
- **Syneresis Rate:** < 1.0% (No weeping)

## 3. Detailed Process Blueprint

### Phase 1: Pectin Hydration and Dispersal (T-Minus 0-2 hrs)
1. **Pre-Mixing:** Mix dry pectin with 50kg sucrose (1:4 ratio) to prevent clumping (lumping).
2. **Hydration Tank:** Prepare 200L RO water in a high-shear mixer. Heat to 85°C.
3. **Dosing:** Gradually add the pectin-sugar mix. Agitate at 3,000 RPM for 20 minutes until a clear, lump-free solution forms.
4. **Viscosity Check:** Use a Brookfield viscometer. Target: 1,500 - 2,500 cP at 80°C.
5. **Holding:** Keep the hydrated pectin solution at 80°C to prevent premature setting.

### Phase 2: Honey Batching and Vacuum Concentration (T-Minus 2-6 hrs)
1. **Sanitization:** CIP all vacuum pans (SS316) with 5% caustic and hot water rinse.
2. **Batch Induction:** Transfer the honey and hydrated pectin into the vacuum evaporator.
3. **Vacuum Control:** Apply 0.7 - 0.8 bar vacuum. This allows boiling at 60-65°C, preventing HMF formation and color darkening.
4. **Thermal Profile:** Maintain a steady heating jacket pressure of 1.5 bar (saturated steam).
5. **Concentration:** Run the batch until the Refractive Index (RI) indicates 63 Brix.

### Phase 3: Acidification and Final Brix Correction (Operations)
1. **Brix verification:** Stop the vacuum. Take a 50ml sample and test solids on a calibrated refractometer. Adjust to 66 Brix ± 1.
2. **pH adjustment:** Gradually meter in Citric acid (50% solution). Target pH is 3.15.
3. **Stability check:** pH is critical; above 3.5, the jelly will not set; below 3.0, the jelly will weep (syneresis).
4. **Flavoring (Optional):** Add natural boosters (lemon/berry) if a hybrid product is desired.
5. **Batch completion:** Heat to 85°C at atmospheric pressure to ensure sterility and proper pectin chain orientation.

### Phase 4: Filling and Headspace Sterilization (Final Ops)
1. **Filling:** Use a rotary piston filler. Standard 250g or 500g glass jars. Fill temp: 82°C ± 2°C.
2. **Capping:** Steam-flow capping (Lugan style) to create a high vacuum inside the jar.
3. **Inversion:** Invert the jars for 120 seconds to sanitize the internal cap surface with hot jelly.
4. **Cooling Tunnel:** Gradually cool from 80°C down to 40°C over 45 minutes using a micro-mist cooling tunnel.
5. **Gelation window:** Pectin network takes 4-24 hours to reach peak bloom strength. Do not disturb during this phase.

### Phase 5: Labeling and Q/A (Post-Production)
1. **Bloom testing:** Take samples from the batch after 24 hrs and test on a Texture Analyzer (Stable Micro Systems).
2. **Incubation:** Hold samples at 37°C for 7 days to check for syneresis or spoilage.
3. **Labeling:** Apply pressure-sensitive labels. Metal detect jars for physical contamination.
4. **Traceability:** Stamp Lot codes with expiration info on the glass neck or cap.

## 4. Machinery & CAPEX Requirements
- **Vacuum Evaporator:** Jacketed SS316 2000L Pan with Scraper Agitator.
- **Pectin Hydrator:** High-Shear Mixer (Silverman or similar).
- **Filling Line:** Automatic Glass Jar Filler/Capper (80 jars/min).
- **Cooling:** Spray Cooling Tunnel (SS316 Body).

## 5. Potential Failure Modes and Mitigations (FMEA)
| Failure Mode | Root Cause | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **No Gel Set** | High PH / Low Soluble Solids | Increase acid titration or continue evaporation. |
| **Syneresis (Weeping)** | pH < 3.0 / High Storage Temp | Buffer pH to 3.25; Optimize HM Pectin ratio. |
| **Air Bubbles** | High Speed Filling / Low Temp | Increase vacuum de-aeration time pre-filling. |

## 7. Utility & Sustainability (Resource Optimization)

- **Utility:** Steam requirement 50kg/hr. Use a shared biomass/gas boiler with the Toffy line.
- **Power:** 25kW peak. Use shared vacuum source to reduce standalone pump costs.
- **Sustainability:** Zero-liquid discharge (ZLD) via secondary treatment of pectin-wash water.

## 9. Production Readiness & QC Checklist (Batch Start)

- **Pre-Flight Check:** Vacuum seal verify (-0.8 bar). Pectin solubility pre-check in lab. Shared boiler pressure (2.5 bar target).
- **MBR Entry:** Traceable Honey Batch ID; Pectin Bloom strength; Cook-temp log.
- **In-Process QC:** Digital Benchtop Refractometer (Target 65-68° Brix). Lab-grade pH meter (3.2 target).
- **Safety Protocol:** High-heat gloves for kettle discharge. Emergency eyewash station check.
- **Kill Switch:** If Brix exceeds 72° (due to over-boiling), stop heat and add controlled diluent immediately.

## 10. Maintenance & Disaster Recovery (West Bengal Specs)

- **Traceability:** Batch QR code links to specific Monod-growth simulation logs for pectin stability.
- **Monsoon Recovery:** Double-wall insulated storage tanks. Humidity-controlled packing room to prevent "Capsule Sweating".
- **Maintenance:** Monthly vacuum pump seal replacement and agitator torque check.
