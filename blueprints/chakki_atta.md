# Chakki Atta (Whole Wheat Flour) Industrial Production: SOP

## 1. Process Overview and Particle Dynamics
Chakki Atta production involves the traditional stone-milling process, adapted for industrial scales to ensure high fiber retention and specific bran-to-germ ratios. The focus is on the "Cold Grinding" technique to prevent thermal damage to vitamins and proteins.

### 1.1 Target Specifications
- **Moisture Content:** 11.5% - 12.5%
- **Ash Content:** 1.2% - 1.5% (High bran retention)
- **Granularity (Pass through 500um):** > 98%
- **Gluten Index:** 75 - 85
- **Water Absorption Power (WAP):** 65% - 70%

## 3. Detailed Process Blueprint

### Phase 1: Grain Intake and Pre-Cleaning (T-Minus 0-2 hrs)
1. **Intake:** Unload wheat from silos onto a high-capacity belt conveyor.
2. **Magnetic Separation:** Pass through 10,000 Gauss rare-earth magnets to remove ferrous contaminants.
3. **Milling Separator:** Remove oversized (sticks, stones) and undersized (sand, seeds) impurities.
4. **De-stoner:** Use gravity-based separation to remove glass and stones.
5. **Scouring:** Pass wheat through a horizontal scourer to remove surface dust and the outermost layer of bran (beeswing).

### Phase 2: Conditioning and Tempering (T-Minus 2-12 hrs)
1. **Moisture Addition:** Spray RO water in a precision dampener to increase moisture by 1.5-2.0%.
2. **Tempering Silos:** Store dampened wheat in tempering bins for 8-12 hours. This toughens the bran and softens the endosperm for uniform grinding.
3. **Pre-Grinding Check:** Moisture must be 14.5% entering the mill for "Cold Grinding" stability.

### Phase 3: Chakki Milling (The Stone Grinding Process)
1. **Feed Control:** Use a volumetric feeder to deliver 1,000 kg/hr per Chakki unit.
2. **Stone Selection:** Use abrasive emery stones (diameter 600mm - 900mm). 
3. **Pressure Regulation:** PLC-controlled stone gap adjustment (microns) based on motor load.
4. **Thermal Management:** Continuous air suction through the milling chamber to keep temperature < 45°C. This is critical for nutritional density.
5. **Bran Integration:** Ensure 100% bran and germ are milled together with the endosperm.

### Phase 4: Sifting and Fiber Verification
1. **Plansifter:** Pass flour through high-capacity gyroscopic sifters (10-12 screens).
2. **Granularity Control:** Target a "Chakki Texture" (Roughness). Over-grinding kills dough elasticity; under-grinding leads to gritty rotis.
3. **Pneumatic Conveying:** Use negative pressure suction to transport flour to the packing silos, providing additional cooling.

### Phase 5: Packaging and Fortification (Final Ops)
1. **Fortification (Optional):** Metered addition of Iron, Folic Acid, and Vit B12 per FSSAI / WHO standards.
2. **Weighing:** Bulk filling into 5kg/10kg HDPE or Multi-layer PET bags.
3. **Metal Detection:** 1.5mm Ferrous / 2.0mm Non-Ferrous sensitivity pass.
4. **Sealing:** Automatic heat sealing + stitching for extra durability.

## 4. Machinery & CAPEX Requirements
- **Mill:** Multiple Traditional Stone Chakki Units (PLC-driven motor control).
- **Cleaner:** Vibro-mill separator with aspiration.
- **Sifter:** Round gyroscopic sifter (3-4 deck).
- **Storage:** SS304 Flour Silos with discharge vibrators.

## 5. Potential Failure Modes and Mitigations (FMEA)
| Failure Mode | Root Cause | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Infestation (Weevils)** | High Moisture / Internal Residue | Monthly heat treatment/fumigation + HEPA air filters. |
| **Protein Denaturation** | Milling Temp > 55°C | Increase air-flow rate or reduce stone pressure. |
| **Gritty Roti** | Large Bran Particles | Adjust stone gap and sifter screen sizes. |

## 6. Regulatory Compliance & Licensing (West Bengal & India)

### 6.1 Central Level (India)
- **FSSAI License:** State or Central License (mandatory for cereal/flour milling).
- **GST Registration:** Mandatory (HSN 1101 for Wheat Flour).
- **Weights & Measures:** PACK (Packaged Commodities) Registration.
- **Factory License:** Required under Factories Act 1948 if using > 10 workers & power.

### 6.2 State Level (West Bengal)
- **WB Pollution Control Board (WBPCB):** "Green" Category CTO.
- **WB Fire & Emergency Services:** Fire NOC (for flour dust/combustible dust safety).
- **Trade License:** From local Municipality (Kolkata/Howrah) or Panchayat.
- **WB Agri-Marketing (Agroweb):** Registration for grain procurement.

### 6.3 Exemptions & Thresholds
- **Factory Act:** Exempt if employing < 10 workers with power or < 20 without power.
- **Pollution NOC:** Exempt for tiny/micro units in approved rural clusters.

## 7. Utility & Sustainability (Resource Optimization)

- **Power Load:** 40kW (Multi-Chakki load). Use Capacitor banks for power factor correction.
- **Sustainability:** Zero-waste milling. Wheat bran and husk sold as organic animal feed.
- **Thermal:** Stone cooling air-cycle avoids protein denaturation without secondary cooling.
