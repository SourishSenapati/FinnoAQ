# Honey Powder Industrial Production: Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

## 1. Process Fundamentals and Thermodynamics
Honey powder (dehydrated honey) is highly hygroscopic due to its fructose and glucose content. Industrial spray drying requires precise control of the glass transition temperature (Tg) to prevent wall deposition and sticking within the dryer.

### 1.1 Target Specifications
- **Moisture Content:** 2.5% - 4.0%
- **Flowability (Hausner Ratio):** 1.10 - 1.25 (Free flowing)
- **Glass Transition (Tg):** 45°C - 55°C
- **Solubility:** > 98% (Tested @ 20°C)
- **Yield Recovery:** 85% - 95% (Stiff Carrier Ratio)

## 3. Detailed Process Blueprint

### Phase 1: Feed Preparation and Homogenization (T-Minus 0-4 hrs)
1. **Solids Mixing:** Dissolve Maltodextrin in warm water (65°C - 70°C) in a jacketed high-shear mixing tank.
2. **Honey Integration:** Gradually add raw honey to the maltodextrin solution. Agitate at 1,500 RPM for 45 minutes to ensure a stable slurry.
3. **Brix Correction:** Measure total solids (Refractive Index). Feed must be at 35 - 40 Brix for optimal atomization.
4. **Surfactant Addition:** Mix Tween 80 into the slurry to reduce surface tension.
5. **Pasteurization:** Heat the feed to 75°C for 3 minutes for microbiological safety.

### Phase 2: Spray Dryer Steady-State Configuration (T-Minus 4-6 hrs)
1. **Sanitization:** CIP the spray chamber (SS316) with 1% alkaline solution and sanitize with peracetic acid.
2. **Pre-Heating:** Run the dryer on water. Standard Inlet Air Temp: 165°C. Stabilize Outlet Air Temp: 75°C - 80°C.
3. **Feed Pumping:** Calibrate the high-pressure feed pump (typically 120-150 bar) to match the evaporation capacity (200 kg/hr).
4. **Dehumification:** Inlet air must be filtered and dehumidified to < 5.0 g/kg moisture to prevent powder clumping.

### Phase 3: Atomization and Drying (Operations)
1. **Atomizer Selection:** Centrifugal Rotary Atomizer (22,000 RPM) or Hydraulic Nozzle (1.5mm orifice).
2. **Droplet Formation:** Target droplet size 15 - 50 microns for rapid evaporation.
3. **Evaporation:** Warm air contacts the droplets, causing instantaneous surface drying and formation of honey-carrier matrix.
4. **Residency Time:** Particle should remain in the drying chamber for 15-25 seconds to reach 3.0% moisture.

### Phase 4: Collection and Cooling (Post-Drying)
1. **Primary Collection:** Powder is separated from the air stream using high-efficiency cyclone separators (98% efficiency).
2. **Secondary Collection:** Fine particles (< 5 microns) are recovered using Pulse-Jet Bag Filters.
3. **Pneumatic Transport:** Powder is transported using cold, dehumidified air (15°C) to prevent local Tg excursions.
4. **Fluidized Bed Cooling:** Pass powder through a vibratory fluidized bed to drop its temp to 25°C before anti-caking dosing.

### Phase 5: Anti-Caking and Packaging (Final Ops)
1. **Dosing:** Metered addition of 0.5% SiO2 through a screw feeder into the final collection hopper.
2. **Sifting:** Pass powder through a 40-mesh sifter to break any minor agglomerates.
3. **Packaging:** Fill into multi-wall paper bags with plastic (LDPE/Al) inner liners using an auger filler.
4. **Sealing:** Heat-seal liners and vacuum-pack for bulk export.

## 4. Machinery & CAPEX Requirements
- **Spray Dryer:** 3-Story Tall Chamber (SS316), Rotary Atomizer, Cyclone, Bag House.
- **Feed System:** Diaphragm pump with Pulse Dampener, jacked supply tanks.
- **Dehumidifier:** Desiccant Wheel (9,000 CFM).
- **Packaging:** Automatic Auger filling machine with VFFS (Vertical Form Fill Seal).

## 5. Potential Failure Modes and Mitigations (FMEA)
| Failure Mode | Root Cause | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wall Deposition** | High Moisture / Inlet Temp Spike | Monitor T-outlet and Tg ratio (T-outlet > Tg + 20). |
| **Agglomeration** | High Humidity in Bagging Area | Maintain Relative Humidity (RH) < 25% in the packing room. |
| **Burnt Particles** | Blocked Atomizer / Residue | Implement camera-based chamber monitoring system. |

## 9. Production Readiness & QC Checklist (Batch Start)

- **Pre-Flight Check:** Atomizer speed (22k RPM); Inlet temp stable (165°C); Bag filter cleanliness check.
- **MBR Entry:** Feed Slurry Batch ID; Carrier-to-Honey ratio; Inlet/Outlet Air Temp logs.
- **In-Process QC:** Rapid Moisture Balance (Target 3.0%). Sieve test for granularity.
- **Safety Protocol:** Anti-static suits + Conductive footwear (Dust explosion risk).
- **Kill Switch:** If Outlet Air Temp hits 90°C (risk of product charring), trip the main heater immediately.

## 10. Maintenance & Disaster Recovery (West Bengal Specs)

- **Monsoon Recovery:** Nitrogen-purged storage for Maltodextrin to prevent lump formation. Zero-humidity "Hygro-Locks" on packing lines.
- **Maintenance:** Bi-weekly atomizer nozzle inspection and pulse-jet solenoid timing audit.
