# Honey Toffy Industrial Production: Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

## 1. Process Overview and Thermal Physics
Honey toffy production involves the thermal concentration and Maillard browning of honey, milk, and fats. The "Hard-Crack" stage (145-155°C) is required to ensure a brittle, shelf-stable confection with low water activity (Aw < 0.2).

### 1.1 Target Specifications
- **Cooking Temperature:** 148°C - 152°C
- **Final Moisture Content:** 1.5% - 2.5%
- **Hardness (Texture Analysis):** 45 N - 60 N
- **HMF (Hydroxymethylfurfural):** < 40 mg/kg (Quality limit)
- **Flavor Profile Score:** 92/100 (No bitterness)

## 3. Detailed Process Blueprint

### Phase 1: Emulsification and Pre-Cooking (T-Minus 0-2 hrs)
1. **Sanitization:** All SS316 2,000L steam-jacketed kettles with scraper-agitators must be sanitized.
2. **Phase Mixing:** Load honey, milk, and salt in a kettle. Heat to 65°C.
3. **Fat Inversion:** Add the घी (Ghee) and Lecithin at 70°C.
4. **Shearing:** Run the high-shear emulsifier (3,000 RPM) for 15 minutes to create a uniform white slurry.
5. **Monitoring:** Verify no "oil slick" is visible on the surface. Emulsion must be stable to 100°C.

### Phase 2: Atmospheric Evaporation and Concentration (T-Minus 2-4 hrs)
1. **Initial Boiling:** Increase steam jacket pressure to 4.0 bar. Heat to 100°C ± 2°C.
2. **De-watering:** Allow 100-150L of water to evaporate as steam.
3. **Scraper Ops:** Continuous scraping (30 RPM) is mandatory to prevent local burning/caramelization on the kettle walls.
4. **Viscosity Tracking:** Torque on the agitator motor monitors the thickening. 

### Phase 3: High-Temperature Caramelization (Final Ops)
1. **Thermal Ramp:** Slowly increase temperature from 125°C (Hard Ball) to 145°C (Soft Crack).
2. **Color Development:** Maillard reactions accelerate. Monitor the "Golden Brown" tint.
3. **Final Target:** Reach 150°C. Hold for 30 seconds for complete moisture flash-off.
4. **Sampling:** A "cold-water drop" sample must shatter instantly when reaching the 150°C setpoint.
5. **Burn Mitigation:** If temperature reaches 158°C, instantly kill the steam and drop the batch to avoid bitterness.

### Phase 4: Slab Cooling and Shaping (Post-Cooking)
1. **Discharge:** Pour the 150°C molten mass onto a water-cooled SS316 slab or into a cooling drum.
2. **Leveling:** Use a heated roller to maintain a thickness of 1.5 cm.
3. **Cooling Cycle:** Drop temperature from 150°C to 85°C over 10 minutes.
4. **Sizing:** Once at 80°C (The plastic/ductile phase), pass through a longitudinal and transverse cutter.
5. **Drop-Former (Optional):** For round toffees, feed to a rotary drop-forming machine.

### Phase 5: Wrapping and Sealing (Final Ops)
1. **Cooling:** Final cooling to 30°C in a dry-air tunnel (RH < 20%).
2. **Sifting:** Remove the "fines" (broken edges) using a vibratory sifter.
3. **Primary Wrap:** High-speed twist wrap or pillow-pack (BOPP/PET metallized) at 300 pieces/min.
4. **Double-Wrap:** If shelf-life > 1 year is needed, uses an outer foil wrap.
5. **Traceability:** Inkjet batch codes and MFG date on the back of each wrap.

## 4. Machinery & CAPEX Requirements
- **Evaporator Kettle:** 2,000L Jacketed SS316 with Scraper Agitator.
- **Cooling Drum:** Water-cooled rotating SS cylinder (2,000 kg/hr).
- **Cutter:** Guillotine or Rotary Cutting unit (Variable speed).
- **Packing:** High-speed pillow-pack machine with N2 flushing.

## 5. Potential Failure Modes and Mitigations (FMEA)
| Failure Mode | Root Cause | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Graininess / Gritty** | Sucrose Recrystallization | Increase honey ratio or add corn/glucose syrup. |
| **Fat Blooming** | Emulsion Failure (Lecithin low) | Increase high-shear mixing time or Lecithin dosage. |
| **Sticking to Wrap** | Residual Moisture > 4% | Increase final cook temp to 152°C or lower RH in packing. |

## 7. Utility & Sustainability (Resource Optimization)

- **Utility:** High steam demand (120kg/hr for boiling/concentration). Shared coal/gas header.
- **Cooling:** Atmospheric cooling slabs + water-cooled drum (Closed loop water recycling).
- **Power:** 20kW (Scraper motors & Conveyors).

## 8. Financial & Tax Efficiency (West Bengal Specs)

- **Incentive:** Fixed Capital Investment Subsidy (FCIS) under WB MSME Policy.
- **Tax Efficiency:** HSN 1704 (Confectionery - 18% GST). Input Tax Credit (ITC) on industrial butter/ghee procurement.
- **Procurement:** Buy-back agreements with local honey cooperatives for price stability.
- **Break-Even (Est):** 14 months (High volume/velocity product).

## 9. Production Readiness & QC Checklist (Batch Start)

- **Pre-Flight Check:** Scraper motor lubrication (H1 Grease). Cooling slab sanitation. Steam valve calibration.
- **MBR Entry:** Batch ID; Glucose-to-Honey ratio; Flavor Lot ID.
- **In-Process QC:** "Hard Crack" test (Temperature probe at 150°C). Moisture Analyzer for final texture.
- **Safety Protocol:** Full face shields for handling molten sugar/honey. Insulated heat-resistant aprons.
- **Kill Switch:** If boiling temperature hits 158°C (risk of charring), activate emergency cold-water jacket.

## 10. Maintenance & Disaster Recovery (West Bengal Specs)

- **Traceability:** QR codes for retail packs mapping the Glasstransition temp during CUDA simulation.
- **Monsoon Recovery:** Dehumidified storage (RH < 45%) mandatory to prevent stickiness/melting in July-August.
- **Maintenance:** Bi-weekly scraper blade inspection for wear & tear.
