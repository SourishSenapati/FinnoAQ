# Sundarban Honey Industrial Processing: SOP

## 1. Process Overview and Moisture Management
Sundarban honey (Multifloral / Mangrove) is known for its high natural moisture content (22-25%) due to the humid delta environment. Industrial processing focuses on "Low-Heat De-moisturization" to reach the 18% moisture limit (FSSAI/EU standards) without degrading the honey's medicinal properties (Phenolics/HMF).

### 1.1 Target Specifications
- **Moisture Content:** 17.5% - 18.5%
- **HMF (Hydroxymethylfurfural):** < 40 mg/kg
- **Diastase Activity:** > 12 Gothe Units
- **Reducing Sugars:** > 65%
- **Clarification:** Ultra-clear (Sparklet filtration).

## 3. Detailed Process Blueprint

### Phase 1: Intake, Pre-Heating, and Filtration (T-Minus 0-4 hrs)
1. **Intake:** Unload raw mangrove honey into a jacketed 10kL intake tank.
2. **Pre-Heating:** Gently warm to 40°C - 45°C. This lowers the viscosity (from ~10,000 cP to < 2,000 cP) for efficient filtering.
3. **Primary Filtration:** Pass through a 100-mesh static filter to remove bee parts, wax, and heavy debris.
4. **Magnetic Separation:** Remove metallic particles from the raw collection process.
5. **Magnetic Field Testing:** Ensure no ferrous contaminants remain in the bulk flow.

### Phase 2: Vacuum Concentration (De-moisturization) (T-Minus 4-12 hrs)
1. **The Vacuum Rule:** Honey must never be heated to > 50°C in open air. 
2. **Setup:** Draw honey into a Falling Film Evaporator or a Vacuum Pan.
3. **Pressure:** Apply 0.85 bar vacuum. At this pressure, water boils at 45°C.
4. **Circulation:** Continuous pumping at 2,000 L/hr to ensure uniform heat transfer and prevent "hot spots" (caramelization).
5. **Condenser Ops:** Efficiently trap the evaporated water (Sundarban honey is 24% moisture; we need to remove approx 600kg water per 10kL).

### Phase 3: Micro-Filtration and Polishing (Operations)
1. **Secondary Filtration:** Once moisture is 18.2%, pass the warm honey through a 150-mesh screen.
2. **Sparklet Filter:** Use a filter press with non-absorbent pads to remove micro-pollen (optional, depends on "Natural" vs "Processed" grade).
3. **De-aeration:** Hold the honey under vacuum in a tank for 2 hours. This removes micro-bubbles that cause "frothing" at the top of the retail jar.
4. **Brix verification:** Confirm final solids percentage (81.5% - 82.5%).

### Phase 4: Cooling and Standardization (Final Ops)
1. **Heat Exchanger:** Rapidly cool the honey from 45°C down to 25°C using a Plate Heat Exchanger (PHE).
2. **Blending:** Mix different batches in a 20kL blending tank to ensure uniform color and flavor profiles.
3. **Batch Analysis:** Lab test for: HMF, Diastase, Sucrose/Fructose ratio, and Antibiotic/Pesticide residues.
4. **HMF Retention:** Maintaining 25°C ensures HMF stays < 10 mg/kg during maturation.

### Phase 5: Bottling and Packaging
1. **Automatic Filling:** Use a high-speed piston filler (accurate to ± 1g). Standard 250g, 500g, 1kg jars.
2. **Capping:** Torque-controlled induction capping for a hermetic seal.
3. **Induction Sealing:** Use Alu-Foil induction liners to prevent leakage and tampering.
4. **Labelling:** Apply premium self-adhesive labels + QR codes for traceability to the specific Sundarban forest range.

## 4. Machinery & CAPEX Requirements
- **Concentrator:** Falling Film Vacuum Evaporator (316L).
- **Filtration:** Rotary Self-Cleaning Filter + Sparklet Filter Press.
- **Tanks:** Jacketed SS316 20kL Silos with agitators.
- **Filling:** High-Capacity Piston Filling Line (80-100 BPM).

## 5. Potential Failure Modes and Mitigations (FMEA)
| Failure Mode | Root Cause | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fermentation** | Moisture > 20% / Yeast activity | Monitor moisture strictly; Heat-shock @ 65°C for 5 min if yeast count high. |
| **High HMF (Darkening)** | Overheating (> 50°C for long) | Use vacuum evaporation; minimize residence time in heaters. |
| **Crystallization** | Seed Crystals / Temperature < 20°C | Ensure full pasteurization of nuclei; Store above 22°C. |
