# Toor Dal (Pigeon Pea) Industrial Production: SOP

## 1. Process Overview and Seed Anatomy
Toor Dal production involves the de-husking and splitting of the pigeon pea (*Cajanus cajan*). The industrial focus is on minimizing broken kernels (chuni) and maximizing the de-hulling efficiency (over 90%) using a multi-stage abrasion process.

### 1.1 Target Specifications
- **Purity Grade:** Grade-I (Min 99.5% whole halves)
- **Broken Kernels (Chuni):** < 0.5%
- **Husk Presence:** < 0.1%
- **Moisture Content:** 10.0% - 11.5%
- **Appearance:** Oily / Non-Oily (User preference).

## 3. Detailed Process Blueprint

### Phase 1: Pre-Cleaning and Grading (T-Minus 0-2 hrs)
1. **Intake:** Unload raw pulses into a large intake hopper.
2. **Pre-Sieving:** Use vibratory screens to remove pods, sticks, and large mud particles.
3. **Magnetic Separation:** Remove ferrous debris pre-milling.
4. **De-stoner:** Use a gravity separator to remove glass, stones, and high-density soil clods.
5. **Grading:** Split seeds into Small, Medium, and Large sizes. Uniform size is critical for high de-husking yields.

### Phase 2: Pitting and Oil Treatment (T-Minus 2-4 hrs)
1. **Dampening:** Increase seed moisture by 2% using atomized spray.
2. **Pitting Unit:** Use an emmery-roller "Pitter" to scratch (pit) the outer husk. This creates entry points for oil and water.
3. **Oiling:** Apply 0.3% oil (Mustard/Linseed) to the pitted pulses in a screw conveyer mixer.
4. **Resting:** Allow seeds to rest for 12-24 hours in temperament silos. This loosens the seed-coat-to-cotyledon bond.

### Phase 3: Drying and Tempering (T-Minus 4-24 hrs)
1. **Solar Drying (Traditional) or Hot Air Drying:** Use a continuous fluid-bed dryer (60°C). 
2. **Moisture Equilibrium:** Target moisture drop from 14% to 10%.
3. **Tempering:** Store in silos for another 12 hours for moisture homogenization. This prevents "Case Hardening."

### Phase 4: De-husking and Splitting (Milling)
1. **De-huller:** Use a Gota machine (Abrasive roller) to strip the loosened husk.
2. **Aspiration:** High-capacity cyclones remove the light husk (bhusa) as a byproduct.
3. **Splitting:** Pass the naked grains through a splitter (Impact or Abrasive).
4. **Separation:** Use a plansifter to separate: Husk, Whole Grain, Split Dal, and Brokens (Chuni).

### Phase 5: Polishing and Packaging (Final Ops)
1. **Water Polishing:** Spray a fine mist of water onto the dal in a screw polisher for a matte finish.
2. **Oil Polishing:** Apply 0.1% oil for a high-gloss finish.
3. **Nylon Polishing:** Pass through a vertical leather-strip or nylon polisher for "Silky" finish.
4. **Color Sorting:** Pass the dal through a CCD-Camera based Color Sorter (Buhler/Satake) to remove discolored or pest-damaged kernels.
5. **Packaging:** Weigh and fill into 500g, 1kg, 2kg PET-laminate pouches.

## 4. Machinery & CAPEX Requirements
- **Mill:** Gota Machine (De-huller) + Pulse Splitter.
- **Cleaner:** De-stoner + Gravity Separator.
- **Sorting:** CCD Color Sorter (High Capacity).
- **Polishing:** Cone Polisher / Leather Polisher.

## 5. Potential Failure Modes and Mitigations (FMEA)
| Failure Mode | Root Cause | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High Broken %** | Low Seed Moisture / High Milling RPM | Tighten temperature control; reduce motor speed. |
| **Poor De-husking** | Inadequate Pitting / Oiling | Increase oil dosage during pitting. |
| **Pest Infestation** | Inadequate Drying (>12% Moisture) | Ensure final moisture is < 11.0%. |
