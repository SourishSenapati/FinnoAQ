# Mustard Oil (Kachi Ghani) Industrial Production: SOP

## 1. Process Overview and Cold-Pressing (Kachi Ghani)
The "Kachi Ghani" process involves the mechanical extraction of oil from mustard seeds using a wood-pressed (Kolhu) mechanism. The goal is to keep the oil temperature below 50°C to preserve Pungency (Allyl Isothiocyanate - AITC) and natural Omega-3 content.

### 1.1 Target Specifications
- **Pungency (AITC):** 0.20% - 0.25% (High grade)
- **Free Fatty Acids (FFA):** < 0.25%
- **Sap Value:** 168 - 175
- **Iodine Value:** 96 - 110
- **Appearance:** Dark yellow to golden tint, clear (Post-sedimentation).

## 3. Detailed Process Blueprint

### Phase 1: Seed Cleaning and Conditioning (T-Minus 0-2 hrs)
1. **Reception:** Feed mustard seeds from silos into a vibratory pre-cleaner.
2. **Magnetic Separation:** Remove metallic debris (nails, screws).
3. **De-stoning:** Eliminate heavy impurities (stones, mud balls).
4. **Moisture Conditioning:** Spray seeds with 2-4% warm water (45°C). This makes the seed husk brittle and improves oil yield in the first press.
5. **Holding:** Let seeds rest for 30 minutes in a conditioning bin.

### Phase 2: Kachi Ghani Extraction (KOLHU UNIT)
1. **Unit Setup:** Use a battery of Wood-Press Kolhus (SS316 chamber with wood-lined pestle/mortar).
2. **Feed Rate:** 200 kg/hr per kolhu.
3. **Milling Speed:** Low RPM (12-16 RPM) to prevent frictional heat.
4. **Thermal Monitoring:** Use IR laser sensors. If oil exits at > 50°C, slow down the pestle rotation.
5. **Extraction:** Cold-pressing breaks the seed cellular structure, releasing raw oil and Deoiled Cake (DOC).

### Phase 3: Crude Oil Filtration and Sedimentation
1. **Primary Collection:** Crude oil flows into an underground collection tank.
2. **Settling:** Allow the oil to rest in sedimentation tanks for 24-48 hours. This allows fine seed particles (sludge) to settle naturally.
3. **Skimming:** Draw the clear top oil layer for final filtration.
4. **Filter Press:** Pass the oil through a vertical plate-and-frame filter press (5-micron cloth).
5. **Transparency Check:** Oil must be crystal clear with no visible suspended particles.

### Phase 4: Pungency Verification and Quality Check (Final Ops)
1. **AITC Lab Analysis:** Titration (GLC method) to verify Allyl Isothiocyanate levels. Target: > 0.20%.
2. **FFA Titration:** Ensure acid value is < 1.0 (FFA < 0.5%).
3. **Bellier Turk Test:** Verify purity (Adulteration check for argemone/palm oil).
4. **Color Index:** Measured on Lovibond scale (Yellow/Red ratio).

### Phase 5: Packaging and Nitrogen Blanketing
1. **Filling:** Use a multi-head piston filler. Standard 1L / 2L PET bottles or 5L tins.
2. **Nitrogen Purging:** Sparge N2 into the bottle pre-filling to displace oxygen.
3. **Capping:** Torque-controlled lug caps or heat-shrunk PET seals.
4. **Labelling:** Automatic rotary labeller + Inkjet batch coding.

## 4. Machinery & CAPEX Requirements
- **Milling:** Battery of 10-15 Wood-Pressed Kolhus.
- **Cleaning:** Vibro-mill separator with aspiration.
- **Filtration:** Stainless steel Filter Press (Frame-and-Plate).
- **Filling:** Automatic High-Speed Bottling Line (60 BPM).

## 5. Potential Failure Modes and Mitigations (FMEA)
| Failure Mode | Root Cause | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Low Pungency** | Overheating (> 55°C) / Old Seeds | Maintain seed storage @ 20°C; reduce RPM. |
| **High Cloudiness** | Poor Filtration / Short Settling | Increase filter cloth density; Extend settling to 72 hrs. |
| **Rancid Odor** | Oxidation in Tank | Oxygen-free storage with N2 blanketing. |

## 9. Production Readiness & QC Checklist (Batch Start)

- **Pre-Flight Check:** Kolhu RPM verification. Filter cloth integrity. Oil collection tank CIP.
- **MBR Entry:** Mustard seed variety (Black/Yellow); Moisture level; Batch ID.
- **In-Process QC:** FFA Titration Kit (Target < 0.25%). Pungency (AITC) level check via sensory/lab.
- **Safety Protocol:** Anti-slip footwear (Oil spill safety). PPE gloves for mechanical adjustment.
- **Kill Switch:** If mechanical noise exceeds 85dB (bearing failure), stop motor instantly.

## 10. Maintenance & Disaster Recovery (West Bengal Specs)

- **Traceability:** Oil pungency levels linked to CUDA-thermal simulation curves.
- **Monsoon Recovery:** Nitrogen-blanketed storage to prevent "Moisture Breath" in tanks. 
- **Maintenance:** Bi-weekly belt tension check and drain-line cleaning.
