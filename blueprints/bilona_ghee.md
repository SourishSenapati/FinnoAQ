# Bilona Ghee (A2 Clarified Butter) Industrial Production: SOP

## 1. Process Overview and Phase Inversion
Bilona Ghee production follows the traditional Vedic method: Curding A2 Milk -> Churning (Bilona) -> Separating Makkhan (Cultured Butter) -> Heating. This process preserves CLA (Conjugated Linoleic Acid) and specific fragrance volatiles (Butyrio/Hexanoic acids).

### 1.1 Target Specifications
- **Fat Content:** > 99.8%
- **Graininess (Danedar):** 1.0mm - 2.0mm granules
- **Free Fatty Acids (FFA):** < 0.3%
- **Clarification Index:** Clear golden-yellow transparency @ 40°C.
- **Microbial Count:** Zero (Thermal elimination).

## 2. Comprehensive Formulation (Per 10,000 L A2 Milk)
| Component | Quantity | Industrial Grade | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A2 Gir/Sahiwal Milk** | 10,000 L | Raw / Un-homogenized | Raw Material (A2 Beta Casein) |

| **Clean Steam** | As needed | Culinary Grade | Thermal Clarification |

## 3. Detailed Process Blueprint

### Phase 1: Milk Pasteurization and Inoculation (T-Minus 0-4 hrs)
1. **Reception:** Transfer raw A2 milk to a holding tank (SS316). 
2. **Heating:** Gently heat to 85°C (HTST) for 15 seconds to kill pathogens while preserving proteins.
3. **Cooling:** Flash-cool to 37°C before inoculation.
4. **Curding:** Add 1% starter culture. Agitate gently for 5 minutes.
5. **Fermentation:** Leave undisturbed for 12-14 hours until pH reaches 4.4 - 4.6 (Solid curd formation).

### Phase 2: The Bilona Process (Churning) (T-Minus 14-16 hrs)
1. **Churning Unit:** Use dual-speed SS-Bilona churners (Reverse/Forward rotation).
2. **Temperature Control:** Maintain curd at 14°C - 16°C. Add chilled water (4°C) gradually.
3. **Phase Inversion:** Mechanical shear breaks the fat globule membrane. Makkhan (butter) begins to float.
4. **Collection:** Skim the cream/butter using a perforated ladle or automated weir.
5. **Washing:** Wash the Makkhan with chilled water to remove buttermilk (Whey/Lactose) till water runs clear.

### Phase 3: Butter Concentration and Melting
1. **Melting Tank:** Transfer washed Makkhan to a double-jacketed SS316 kettle.
2. **Heating Ramp:** Gradually increase temperature to 70°C to melt the fat.
3. **Water Removal:** Evaporate residual moisture under gentle boiling.
4. **Scraper Ops:** Use a low-speed anchor agitator with Teflon scrapers to prevent protein burning on the walls.

### Phase 4: Clarification and Flavor Development (Final Ops)
1. **The "Danedar" Stage:** Increase temperature to 118°C - 122°C. 
2. **Phase Separation:** Casein/Lactose solids (Khoya) sink to the bottom. Pure Ghee floats on top.
3. **Aroma Formation:** Hold at 120°C for 2 minutes for the "Nutty" aroma (Maillard reaction between trace proteins and fats).
4. **Filtration:** Pass the hot ghee through a 5-micron SS mesh filter or a centrifuge to remove charred protein solids.

### Phase 5: Granulation and Packaging
1. **Cooling:** Gradually cool from 120°C to 45°C. Rapid cooling prevents granule formation.
2. **The Seeding Rule:** Maintain at 30°C for 5-8 hours. This allows large beta-prime fat crystals to grow, creating the "Danedar" texture.
3. **Bottling:** Fill into sterilized glass or food-grade PET jars.
4. **Nitrogen Flushed:** Seal under N2 blanketing to ensure 12-18 months of oxidative stability.

## 4. Machinery & CAPEX Requirements
- **Churner:** High-capacity Bilona Churn (SS316).
- **Curding Tanks:** Insulated fermentation silos (10kL).
- **Melting Kettle:** Steam-jacketed with Scraper (2,000L).
- **Clarification:** Horizontal Centrifuge or Filter Press.

## 5. Potential Failure Modes and Mitigations (FMEA)
| Failure Mode | Root Cause | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High FFA (Rancid)** | Over-fermentation / Lipase activity | Control curd pH strictly; Heat kill post-curding. |
| **Loss of Aroma** | Low Finishing Temperature (<115°C) | Ensure final temperature reaches 120°C-122°C. |
| **Greasy Texture** | Too Rapid Cooling | Implement slow-cooling gradient (1°C per 15 min). |

## 6. Regulatory Compliance & Licensing (West Bengal & India)

### 6.1 Central Level (India)
- **FSSAI License:** State License (Dairy sector specific).
- **BIS (IS 11623):** Code for hygienic conditions for dairy plants.
- **GST Registration:** Mandatory (HSN 0405 for Ghee).
- **AGMARK:** Compulsory to use the "Ghee" label in mass retail.

### 6.2 State Level (West Bengal)
- **WB Pollution Control Board (WBPCB):** "Green" Category CTO.
- **WB Fire & Emergency Services:** Fire NOC for steam boilers/melting kettles.
- **Trade License:** From local Municipal Corporation.
- **Animal Resources Development (ARD) Dept:** For milk sourcing verification.

### 6.3 Exemptions & Thresholds
- **FSSAI:** Registration (instead of License) if turnover < ₹12 Lakhs.
- **Pollution NOC:** Exempt for household "Cottage" scale Bilona units (single churner).
